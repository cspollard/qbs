# What `Continuous/Monad` requires, categorically

Setting: `𝒞 = QBSCat S` is cartesian closed, `ℛ = ℛQ` is the representable of the random source, `AQ = [0,1]` is the
object of test values, and `A` is the (Ω-)semiring of integrals.  (Notation: `[X,Y]` is the exponential.)

## 1. Input data

1. **Measures on ℛ** (a class `K`): a presheaf `M` on the measurable maps `Rˢ → Rˢ`, with the action
   `f ↦ f_*` (pushforward; `ok-push`).  It has a Dirac element `δ` and a bind `M(ℛ) × (ℛ ⇒ M(ℛ)) → M(ℛ)`
   (`ok-bind`), the Kleisli extension of the monad `M` on measurable spaces.
2. **Integration**, natural in ℛ: a pairing `∫ : M(ℛ) × 𝒞(ℛ, AQ) → A`, with `∫ (f_*μ) h = ∫ μ (h ∘ f)`.
   It is monotone in the measure and respects pointwise equality of the test function.
3. **Tonelli**: `∫ (bind μ k) h = ∫ μ (r ↦ ∫ (k r) h)`.  Measurability of the integrand `r ↦ ∫ (k r) h` is the
   fact that `∫ᶠ` lands in `AQ`.
4. **The bit-splitting isomorphism** `ℛ ≅ ℛ × ℛ` in 𝒞 (`fromH`/`toH`), which lets `bind` be stated for
   measures on ℛ while the kernel depends on two copies of ℛ.

## 2. The construction

* **Carrier.**  `T X = ∫^ℛ M(ℛ) × 𝒞(ℛ, X)`, the coend: pairs `[α, μ]` (a probe and a measure) modulo pushforward.
  Concretely it is the image of `[α, μ] ↦ (h ↦ ∫ μ (h ∘ α))` in `[[X, AQ], A]`: two pairs are equal exactly when
  every test `h : X → AQ` integrates to the same value (`_≈ᵖ_`; `Pre` is the representative, `PreS` the setoid).
  So `T X` is a *subobject of the continuation object* `[[X, AQ], A]`, and `∫ : T X → [[X, AQ], A]` is mono.
* **Probes.**  `β : ℛ → T X` is random iff it is a parametrised coend element: a *joint probe* `a : ℛ × ℛ ⇒ X` and
  a measurable kernel `g : ℛ → M(ℛ)`, with `β r = [a(r, –), g r]` (`Wit`; the section `sec a r` is `a(r, –)`).
  Equivalently `Probes(T X)(ℛ) = ∫^{ℛ′} M(ℛ′) × 𝒞(ℛ × ℛ′, X)`.  "Measurable kernel" is `KernelOk g`.
  The family is a presheaf (closed under `f_*` on the parameter) and glues along countable partitions.
* **Functor.**  `T f [α, μ] = [f ∘ α, μ]`.
* **Unit.**  `η x = [const x, δ]`.
* **Join.**  A measure on measures `[β, μ]`, with `β` random given by `(a, g)`, goes to
  `[a ∘ fromH, bind μ g′]`, where `g′` is the kernel `g` composed with the split of the sample (`JoinKernel`,
  `P₁.pushMerge`).

## 3. Why it is a monad

`∫ : T → Cont` (with `Cont X = [[X,AQ],A]`) is componentwise mono, and the three equations
`∫-map`, `∫-η`, `∫-join` say that `∫` commutes with `T f`, `η`, `join` and their continuation counterparts.
Therefore `T` is a **submonad of the continuation monad** with the monad laws inherited from `Cont`
(`IntegralMonad.Laws`, which turns this into the agda-categories `Monad`).  Beyond the equations, what must be
proved is *closure*: `T f`, `η` and `join` send random elements to random elements.  That is what `Wit`
(`a`, `g`, `g-ok`, `g-pr`, `w-eq`) and `JoinWit` exist for.

## 4. Variants

* **Probability / sub-probability**: change only the measure class `K` (`MeasureClass`).
* **s-finite** (`SFinite`): the same construction with `W × X` in place of `X`, tests `X → W` (weights `WQ`), and
  integrals `J` of unbounded functions.  It is `T_prob (W × –)`, a writer-like composite, with Tonelli for `join`.

## 6. A categorical interface (sketch)

One module would take the data of §1 (a presheaf `M` of measures with `δ`, bind, pushforward, integration and
Tonelli, plus the splitting iso) and give `T`, `η`, `join`, and `T X ↪ Cont X` for free.  The class variants
and the s-finite case would be instances.  The hand-written `Wit` would become the single statement "`T X` is
the sheaf/presheaf of probes generated by pieces `[a(r,–), g r]`", which is what `fromPieces`/`fromPred` build.

### Not in agda-categories (would have to be written)

* **The continuation monad** `Cont X = [[X, AQ], A]` for a cartesian closed category (§3).  It is the target of
  the monad morphism `∫`.  Only the pieces (exponentials in `Category.CartesianClosed`, `Monad`) exist.
* **A "submonad" notion**: `Monad⇒` plus a proof that each component is mono.  Nothing packages this, so it
  would be a small local record.
* **Day convolution / a monad generated by a monoid-like object** (the shape of `join` via `ℛ ≅ ℛ × ℛ`): no
  construction in the library, so `join` would stay a direct definition.
* **Measures themselves** (pushforward, Dirac, bind, Tonelli) and the quotient of pairs by equal integrals:
  these are this library's own `Analysis/*` and `IntegralMonad`.

### Consequence for the refactor

**The coend is not `T X`.**  An experiment on the discrete model built the bifunctor
`(R,R′) ↦ Probes(X)(R) × Weights(R′)` and the cowedge into `PreS X` (dinaturality is `push-∑`).  The `Coend`
record's universal property does not hold: `PreS X` identifies pairs with equal integrals, which is coarser than the
equivalence generated by `(α∘f, μ) ~ (α, f_*μ)`.  Two pairs with equal integrals need a common refinement `β`, `f₁`,
`f₂` with `f₁_*μ₁ = f₂_*μ₂`, i.e. matching fibres of `α₁`, `α₂` in `X`, which needs choice.  So `T X` is the **image**
of the pairs in `[[X,AQ],A]` (Kock's embedding into the double dual), not the coend.  The experiment was discarded.

**What replaced it** (`QBS/Integration.agda`).  An *integration* is a natural transformation `∫ : M ⇒ Dual Test V`
on the category 𝕄 of measurable maps, where `Test` is the presheaf of test functions, `M` the covariant functor of
measures (pushforward), and `Dual Test V = Hom(–, V) ∘ Testᵒᵖ` the double dual.  Naturality is
`∫ (f_*μ) h = ∫ μ (h∘f)`.  `RawIntegration` + `fromRaw` build one from raw measures, a `push`, and the single
projection formula; `M` is then raw measures modulo equal integrals, and its functor laws are inherited from `Dual`.
Instances: `Model/Integration` (the projection formula is `push-∑`) and `Continuous/Integration` (it is `refl`).
Dirac, bind and Tonelli are not yet part of the record; they will be added when `join` needs them.

**Consumer.**  `Integration.Pairs` (pairs of a probe and a raw measure, equal when every test integrates equally)
now defines `Pre`, `∫` and `PreS` for `Continuous/Monad`, which typechecks again on the migrated core (`TQ` is built
by `fromPred`; `witOf`/`rwit` convert between `Random (TQ X)` and `Wit`).
