# Public interface

This page lists the names a user imports, grouped by module, with one line of meaning each. It is a map, not a
tutorial: for a first read start with `Guide.md`, and for the module-by-module overview see `Modules.md` (generated).
Open the module for full types.

How to read an entry: `name` — meaning. A name in parentheses after the dash, such as `(module X lem₁)`, says which
parameterised module it lives in; `lem₁ : LEM₁` is the excluded-middle hypothesis (see `Classical.md`). Conventions:
`ℓ` is a universe level, `S` a `RandomSource`, `Hom S X Y` a morphism in `QBSCat S`. `scripts/doccheck.py` checks
that every module path below exists and every name still occurs in it.

## Abstract layer

### `QBS.Core`
- `idH` — `idH S {X}`: the identity morphism (`S` explicit).
- `RandomSource` — a one-object site: `Measurability` (a setoid `Rˢ` with carrier `R` and equality `≈ᴿ`, and `Meas`, a monoid of setoid maps `Func Rˢ Rˢ` closed under `≈` and containing the constants), `Partitions` (`Idx`, the probes of discrete `ℕˢ`, along which `Meas` glues: `meas-glue`) and `NatPairing` (`pair`/`unpair`) plus `idx-glue` (covers compose); their fields are re-exported.
- `Probes` — `Probes M X`: a predicate `Random` on setoid maps `Func Rˢ X`, closed under `≈` (`random-resp`), constants and precomposition by `Meas`; `measProbes` is `Meas` itself (the representable); `random-glue-∘` says gluing commutes with postcomposition by a setoid map, and `random-glue-pre` that gluing along `j` then precomposing with `γ` is gluing the precomposed family along `j ∘ γ`.
- `QBS` — a quasi-Borel space: a setoid `Carrier` with `probes` that glue along partitions (`glue-random`, the sheaf condition). The core never assumes `≡` on `R`: each source chooses its equality.
- `CodiscQ` — `CodiscQ S A`: the codiscrete QBS on a setoid (every sequence random); the terminal object, the weights object and `Disc` of the examples are instances.
- `GluedQBS` — the QBS generated by a presheaf of pieces (pieces `P`, `ev`, `pre`, `ev-pre`, constant pieces): random iff locally a piece; `_+Q_`, `CoeqQ`, `ΣQ` and `DiscQ` are instances.
- `Hom` — a setoid map `map` preserving random elements (`map-random`).
- `glueₛ`, `constₛ`, `⇑`, `_∘ₛ_`, `ℕˢ` — gluing a family of setoid maps along an index map, constant maps, any function out of a `≡`-setoid, composition, discrete ℕ. `refineₛ j i` (in `NatPairing`) is the composite cover of `j` by the `i n`.

### `QBS.Category`, `QBS.Limits`, `QBS.Colimits`, `QBS.Complete`, `QBS.Exponential`
- `QBSCat` — `QBSCat S`, the category (agda-categories `Category`); `QBS.Category.Forget`: `Forget` and `Forget-faithful`, the faithful forgetful functor to `Setoids`; `QBS.Category.Concrete`: `QBSSub` (the `SubCategory` of `Setoids` of probe-preserving maps) with the strong equivalence `QBS≃Sub`, the codiscrete and discrete QBS `CodiscQ`/`DiscQ`, the adjoint triple `Disc⊣Forget`, `Forget⊣Codisc`, and the preservation theorems `Forget-continuous`, `Forget-cocontinuous`, `Codisc-continuous`, `Disc-cocontinuous`; `QBS.Category.Cartesian`: `cartesian`, `V`, `braided`, the cartesian, cartesian-monoidal and braided structures of `QBSCat S` (module parameter `S`); `QBS.Category.Measurable`: `MeasCat` (measurable maps as a one-object `SubCategory`) and `yoneda : MeasCat → QBSCat S` (object ↦ `ℛQ`), full and faithful, with `Forget∘yoneda≡Sub`.
- `QBS.Exponential`: `cartesianClosed` — `CartesianClosed (QBSCat S)`; `QBS.Limits`: terminal object and products; `QBS.Colimits`: coproducts.
- `QBS.Complete`: `countableProduct`, `countableCoproduct` — indexed products and coproducts.

### `QBS.Probes`
- `ℛQ` — the probe object, the space of random sources; random elements of `X` correspond to morphisms `ℛQ ⇒ X`.

### Abstract layer map
The dependency stack is `Core → Category → {Exponential, Limits, Colimits, Complete} → Probability → Statistical → IsQBSLike → IsStatisticalQBSLike → Instance`.  `Probability` and `Statistical` are stated over an arbitrary cartesian closed category `C` and do not mention QBS; `QBSCat S` is the primary instance (`Instance`), and the discrete and continuous models supply the monads.  `IsQBSLike` bundles what an example needs: cartesian closure, (countable) coproducts and products, a probe object, and a `ProbabilityInterface`.  Two generic helpers sit under `Probability`: `QBS.CommMonad` (`ProbMonad CC`, the one spelling of "commutative monad on a cartesian closed category", with `braidedOf`) and `QBS.StrongPoint` (`ψ`, `σ-point`, `τ-point`, `ψ-point`: strength of any commutative monad at global elements; later modules should reuse it).  Bind is named once, in `QBS.Bind` (`bind k t = μ ∘ M k ∘ t`); later modules should use it.

### `QBS.CommMonad`, `QBS.StrongPoint`, `QBS.Bind`
- `braidedOf`, `ProbMonad` — the braided monoidal structure of the products of a cartesian closed category, and commutative monads for it.
- `Strength-at-points` — `ψ` (Kock's double strength), `σ-unit`, `τ-unit`, `σ-point`, `τ-point`, `ψ-point`; generic, no ℛ or measures.
- `Bind M` (module `Bind`, for any monad `M`) — `bind k t = μ ∘ M k ∘ t`, Kleisli composition `_∘ᴷ_`, and the laws `bind-return`, `bind-η`, `bind-η-∘` (units), `bind-assoc`, `∘ᴷ-assoc`, `bind-pre`, `bind-nat`, `bind-map`, `bind-join`, `bind-cong`; `scaleM a t` is `bind t (score ∘ a)`. Use it instead of writing `μ ∘ M k ∘ t`.

### `QBS.Probability`
- `ProbabilityInterface` — an abstract commutative strong monad of probability measures over a cartesian closed category with probe object `ℛ`, assembled from the monad (`ProbMonad`), `ProbLaws` and `Fubini` (fields re-exported).
- `Measure` — the type of probability measures on `ℛ`; `measure μ : ⊤ ⇒ T ℛ` embeds a measure as a global element of the monad, and `law a μ = T a ∘ measure μ : ⊤ ⇒ T X` (derived) is the distribution of a probe `a : ℛ ⇒ X` under `μ`.
- `measure-push` (pushforward is `T m`), `measure-total` (mass one), `law-surjective` — the axioms linking measures to the monad; `law-resp`, `law-map`, `law-pre`, `law-return` are derived from them; `split`, `tensor`, `measure-tensor` (`T split.from ∘ measure (tensor μ ν) ≈ ψ ∘ ⟨measure μ, measure ν⟩`, with `ψ` the monoidal structure of the commutative monad) — the Fubini axiom; `kernel`, `kernel-law`, `law-join` are derived from it.

### `QBS.Statistical`
`StatisticalInterface` on top of a `ProbabilityInterface`, assembled from five small structures (their fields are re-exported, so `open StatisticalInterface St` still gives every name), plus `scaled-laws` (every measure is a countable sum of scaled laws):
- `CommutativeMonoidWithZero` (`weights`): `W`, `mulW`, `oneW`, `zero⇒`, and `mul-assoc`, `mul-comm`, `mul-one`, `mul-zero`.
- `SubMonad` (`measures`): `commutativeMonad` (the measure monad `M`), `inclusion` (Prob into `M`), `inclusion-strong`.
- `Score` (`scoring`): `zero` (zero measure), `score : W ⇒ M 1`, `score-zero`, `score-one`, `score-mul`, `zero-bind`.  It *defines* `scaleM a t = μ ∘ M t ∘ score ∘ a` and proves `scale-one`, `scale-mul`, `scale-return`, `scale-nat`, `scale-join`.
- `CountableSums` (`sums`): `sumK` (sums of kernels), `sumK-point`, `bind-sumK`, `sum-nat`, `sum-join`; `sumM` is `sumK` on global measures.  `scale-sum` is derived from `bind-sumK`.
- `Normalization` (`normalization`): `normalize : ∀ X → M X ⇒ Prob X + 1`, `normalize-prob`, `normalize-zero`, `normalize-scale`.

### `QBS.StatisticalLemmas`
- `bind-sum`, `bind-scale` — bind preserves countable sums and scaling of the measure argument.

### `QBS.IsQBSLike`, `QBS.IsStatisticalQBSLike`, `QBS.Instance`
- `IsQBSLike`, `IsStatisticalQBSLike` — the minimal structure for probability, resp. soft conditioning.
- `isQBSLike S P`, `isStatisticalQBSLike S P St` — package any random source with a (hypothesised) monad.

## Discrete model

### `QBS.Model.Semiring`
- `CompleteSemiring` — a commutative semiring with countable sums `∑` (fields `_+_`, `_*_`, `0#`, `1#`, `∑`).
- `NormalizableSemiring` — a complete semiring with inverses for normalisation (`complete` is the underlying semiring).

### `QBS.Model.Monad`
- `Measures` — `module Measures A K`: measures over `A` of weight class `K`.
- `Pre` — a discrete pre-measure: probe `α`, weights `μ`, proof `μ-ok`; `mk` builds one.
- `∫` — `∫ p f`: the expectation `∑ r. μ r * f (α r)`.
- `ηPre`, `mapPre`, `joinPre`, `∫-η`, `∫-join` — unit, map, join and their integral rules.

### `QBS.Model.WeightClasses`
- `probClass`, `allClass` — total mass 1 / arbitrary weights; `QBS.Model.SubProb`: `subClass` — total mass ≤ 1.

### `QBS.Model.Discrete`, `QBS.Model.StatInstance`
- `discreteStatisticalQBSLike` — `NormalizableSemiring → IsStatisticalQBSLike`, the discrete model.
- `discreteStat` (`StatInstance`) — its `StatisticalInterface`, with `normalize`.

### Concrete semirings: `QBS.Model.BoolSemiring`, `QBS.Model.ViterbiSemiring`, `QBS.Model.AdditiveSemiring`, `QBS.Model.AdditiveModel`, `QBS.Model.BoolModel`, `QBS.Model.ViterbiModel`
- `boolComplete`, `additiveComplete`, `additiveNormalizable` — complete / normalisable semirings (Boolean, additive `[0,∞]`); models `QBS.Model.BoolModel`, `ViterbiModel`, `AdditiveModel` (`additiveStatisticalQBSLike`).

## Number foundations

Numbers come in three layers: (1) `Omega`, an ω-continuous ordered semiring of values (an ω-cpo with `+` and `×`
Scott-continuous); (2) `Archimedean`/`Min`/`Series`/`SeriesSup`/`Unbounded`, constructions and extra axioms stated
abstractly in an `OmegaSemiring`, in the order `OmegaSemiring` → `Arch` → `Min` → `Series` → `Unbounded`; (3)
`LowerReals`/`LowerRealsArch`/`LowerRealsMin`/`UnboundedLowerReals`, the lower-cut instance, which is the only place the
rationals appear.  `RationalLemmas` is the adapter layer between stdlib ℚᵘ lemmas and the cut proofs.

## Continuous model

### `QBS.Borel.Source`
- `cantorSource` — Cantor space `2^ℕ` as a `RandomSource`.
- `measₛ`, `idxₛ` — a measurable map or a partition as a setoid map (Borel sets respect pointwise equality).

### `QBS.Continuous.Sample`, `QBS.Analysis.CoinIntegral`, `QBS.Analysis.Omega`
- `UnitInterval` (`Sample`) — `module UnitInterval A`: the unit interval as a QBS `AQ`; `S` — the Cantor random source used by the continuous model.
- `lemOf` (`CoinIntegral`) — turns `lem₁` into the `lem` the lower-cut construction needs; `LowerRealsOmega (lemOf lem₁)` is the value semiring.
- `OmegaSemiring` (`Omega`) — the value-semiring record (`_≈_`, `1#`, order, suprema).

### `QBS.Analysis.Measure`, `QBS.Analysis.Fun`
- `Integration` (`Measure`) — `module Integration A`: `probClass` (probability measures of the continuous model; not the discrete `WeightClasses.probClass`).
- `Measure` — a sub-probability measure as a Daniell functional on measurable functions; `IsProb` — total mass 1.
- `Fun` — the inductive class of measurable `[0,1]`-valued functions on Cantor space.

### `QBS.Analysis.CoinIntegral`, `QBS.Continuous.FairCoin`
- `fairCoin` — the fair-coin measure on Cantor space; `fairCoin-cyl` — its value on cylinders.

### `QBS.Analysis.Meas`, `QBS.Analysis.Lebesgue`
- `Meas` — threshold measurability of `[0,∞]`-valued functions; `Lebesgue`: `J m φ` — the integral of a `Meas` function (unbounded).

### `QBS.Analysis.Cauchy`, `QBS.Analysis.Exp`, `QBS.Analysis.ExpLower`, `QBS.Analysis.NegExp`
The exponential on `[0,∞]` (groundwork for `e^{−x}` and exponential distributions).
- `⨆-*-lub`, `diag`, `tri`, `rect`, `cauchy` (`Cauchy`) — `module Cauchy A`: `(∑ᶠ a) * (∑ᶠ b) ≈ ∑ᶠ (n ↦ diag g n 0)`, the Cauchy product of two series, with `g i j = a i * b j`.
- `exp`, `ι`, `term`, `exp-mono`, `exp-cong`, `exp-zero`, `1+≤exp`, `exp-add` (`Exp`) — `module Exp A`, `module WithInv inv inv-ok`: `exp x = ∑ᶠ (xⁿ/n!)` given reciprocals `inv n * natE (n+1) ≈ 1`; `exp (x + y) ≈ exp x * exp y`; also `exp≤3 : x ≤ 1 → exp x ≤ 3`, `exp≤three^ : x ≤ natE N → exp x ≤ 3ᴺ` and `exp≤quad : x ≤ 1 → exp x ≤ 1 + (x + x·x)`.
- `expC`, `expC-zero`, `expC-mono`, `expC-cong`, `1+≤expC`, `expC≤quad`, `qC-mul`, `pow3`, `expC-bound` (`ExpLower`) — the instance at lower cuts, with `inv n = qC (1/(n+1))`; `expC-bound : x ⊑ natE N → ∀ s ∈ expC x, s < pow3 N` (`3ᴺ`).
- `negExp`, `negExp-anti`, `negExp-zero`, `negExp≤1`, `negExp-expC≤1`, `near-max`, `oneC≤negExp-expC`, `negExp-expC`, `negExp-expC-nat`, `Meas-expC`, `Meas-negExp` (`NegExp`, `module NegExp lem`) — `e^{−x}` as a cut: `q ∈ negExp x` iff `∃ p > q, ∀ s ∈ expC x, p·s < 1`; antitone, `negExp 0 ≋ 1`, `negExp x ⊗ expC x ⊑ 1`, and (`oneC≤negExp-expC`, `negExp-expC`) `negExp x ⊗ expC x ≋ 1` whenever `expC x` is bounded by a rational `B` (`∀ s ∈ expC x, s < B`), and unconditionally for `x ⊑ natE N` (`negExp-expC-nat`); measurable in `x`.

### `QBS.Analysis.Signed`, `QBS.Analysis.SignedOmega`, `QBS.Analysis.SignedIntegral`, `QBS.Analysis.Variance`
Signed values and signed integrals (plan 2, step 1). No cancellation or classical principle in `Signed`.
- `SubSemiring` (`Signed`) — a subsemiring of "finite" elements; `Signed` — `module Signed R F`: the ring of differences of finite elements (Grothendieck construction).
- `Sg` — a signed value `pos − neg` with finite parts; `_≃_` equality (`p + n′ + k ≈ p′ + n + k` for finite `k`); `_+S_`, `_*S_`, `-S_`, `0S`, `1S`, `ι` (embedding), `signedRing` (a `CommutativeRing`), `ι-+`, `ι-*`.
- `Bounded` (`SignedOmega`) — `module Bounded A`: finite = bounded by a natural number (`Bnd`, `nat`); re-exports `Signed` at that subsemiring.
- `Var pm f b`, `var-formula` (`Variance`) — variance `E[(f−E f)²]` of a bounded signed function, and `Var f ≃ E[f²] − (E f)²` (`_*SF_[_]` multiplies bounded signed functions).
- `SF m` (`SignedIntegral`) — a signed integrable function against `m`: measurable parts `f⁺`, `f⁻` with bounded integrals; `E` — its signed integral; `_≈SF_`, `E-cong`.
- `_+SF_`, `E-+` — sum and additivity; `-SF_`, `E-neg`; `scaleSF`, `E-scale` — scaling by a signed constant; `constSF`, `E-const`, `J-const` — constants against a probability measure.

### `QBS.Continuous.Monad`, `QBS.Continuous.ProbInstance`, `QBS.Continuous.SubProb`, `QBS.Continuous.RealModel`
- `Continuous` (`Monad`) — `module Continuous A lem K` (value semiring, `lem`, measure class such as `Integration.probClass`): the probability monad: `Pre`, `mk`, `∫`, `joinPre`, `∫-join`.
- `ContinuousProb` (`ProbInstance`) — it is a `ProbabilityInterface`; `continuousQBSLike`; `RealModel`: `realQBSLike`.
- `ContinuousSub` (`SubProb`) — the sub-probability monad; `scoreH` — soft conditioning by a measurable likelihood in `[0,1]`.
- `QBS.Analysis.Cone` (`Cone A`) — measures as a cone: `zeroM`, `scaleM c c≤1 μ`, `addM μ₁ μ₂ (mass μ₁ + mass μ₂ ≤ 1)`; `Mixture.mix` is `addM` of two `scaleM`s.

### `QBS.Continuous.SignedTests`, `QBS.Inference.ExpectationResp`, `QBS.Inference.Expectation`, `QBS.Inference.ExpectationProduct`, `QBS.Inference.ExpectationInt`, `QBS.Inference.ExpectationIntProduct`, `QBS.Inference.ExpectationExamples`, `QBS.Inference.BoolDist`, `QBS.BoolQ`, `QBS.Inference.CoinUniformPrior`, `QBS.Inference.CoinPosterior`, `QBS.Inference.CoinUniformPriorN`, `QBS.Inference.BernHalf`, `QBS.Inference.CoinBayesRisk`
Expectations of bounded signed statistics under the monad's measures (module parameter `lem₁ : LEM₁`).
- `STest X` (`SignedTests`) — a bounded signed test `pos − neg` (parts `Hom X CutQ`, common natural bound); `pullback`; `E-at p g` (`ExpectationResp`) — its expectation under `p : Pre X`, a signed value; `E-resp` — `p ≈ᵖ q → E-at p g ≃ E-at q g`.
- `_+T_`, `-T_`, `scaleT c`, `constT c`, `precompT f`, `valT g x` (`Expectation`) — the algebra of signed tests.
- `E-at-η` — `E-at (ηPre x) g ≃ valT g x`; `E-at-map` — `E-at (mapPre f p) g ≃ E-at p (precompT f g)`.
- `E-at-+`, `E-at-neg`, `E-at-scale`, `E-at-const` — linearity in the test and constants.
- `expT g`, `E-at-expT`, `E-at-join` — `q ↦ E-at q g` as a signed test on `TQ X`, and the tower law `E-at (joinPre P) g ≃ E-at P (expT g)`.
- `f ⊗T g`, `valT-⊗T` (`ExpectationProduct`) — the product of signed tests on `X ×Q Y`, with `valT (f ⊗T g) (x , y) ≃ valT f x *S valT g y`; `E-at-cong` — pointwise `≃` tests have `≃` expectations.
- `E-at-σ` — `E-at (σPre (x , q)) (f ⊗T g) ≃ valT f x *S E-at q g`; `p ⊗ᵖ q` — the product measure `joinPre (mapPre (σq q) p)`; `E-at-⊗` — independence, `E-at (p ⊗ᵖ q) (f ⊗T g) ≃ E-at p f *S E-at q g`.
- `ITest p`, `E-atᴵ p g`, `fromSTest`, `E-atᴵ-fromSTest`, `truncHom`, `J-resp∞`, `transportI`, `E-respᴵ`, `_+I_`, `-I_`, `scaleI`, `E-atᴵ-+`, `E-atᴵ-neg`, `E-atᴵ-scale`, `valI`, `E-atᴵ-η`, `precompI`, `E-atᴵ-map` (`QBS.Inference.ExpectationInt`) — expectations of integrable (possibly unbounded) signed statistics: `J-resp∞ : p ≈ᵖ q → J (m p) (h ∘ α p) ≈ J (m q) (h ∘ α q)` (via the truncations `truncHom h k`), `E-respᴵ : p ≈ᵖ q → E-atᴵ p g ≃ E-atᴵ q (transportI e g)`.
- `Jp p h`, `J-join`, `J-σ`, `J-⊠`, `J-prodH`, `_⊗I_`, `E-atᴵ-⊗` (`ExpectationIntProduct`) — independence for integrable tests: `J-join` is the unbounded `∫-join` (Tonelli for `joinPre`), `J-⊠ : Jp (p ⊗ᵖ q) (mulX u v) ≈ Jp p u * Jp q v` for unbounded `u, v ≥ 0`, `f ⊗I g : ITest (p ⊗ᵖ q)` (parts `f⁺g⁺ + f⁻g⁻`, `f⁺g⁻ + f⁻g⁺`), and `E-atᴵ-⊗ : E-atᴵ (p ⊗ᵖ q) (f ⊗I g) ≃ E-atᴵ p f *S E-atᴵ q g`.
- `idT`, `sqT : STest AQ`, `E-at-uniform-id`, `E-at-uniform-sq`, `bern : Pre AQ`, `E-at-bern`, `E-at-bern²`, `E-at-bern²-quarter` (`QBS.Inference.ExpectationExamples`) — worked examples: `E-at uniform idT ≃ ι ½`, `E-at uniform sqT ≃ ι m` (`3m = 1`), Bernoulli(½) as the first bit of the fair coin with `E-at bern idT ≃ ι ½`, and independence `E-at (bern ⊗ᵖ bern) (idT ⊗T idT) ≃ ι ½ *S ι ½` (four copies add up to 1).
- `BoolQ`, `boolQ`, `trueQ`, `falseQ`, `constQ`, `ifQ a b` (`QBS.BoolQ`) — the Boolean QBS `1Q +Q 1Q` and case analysis `ifQ a b : Hom BoolQ Z` (the `Colimits.copair` of two constants); unlike `CodiscQ Bool` its indicators are measurable.
- `bitB`, `bernB : Pre BoolQ`, `ifT a b`, `indT : STest BoolQ`, `E-at-bernB-if`, `E-at-bernB-ind`, `toAQ`, `bernB≈bern`, `E-at-bernB-id`, `indB`, `bernθ θ`, `E-at-bernθ` (`QBS.Inference.BoolDist`) — Bernoulli(½) on `BoolQ` with `E-at bernB (ifT a b) ≃ ½a + ½b`, `E-at bernB indT ≃ ι ½`, `mapPre toAQ bernB ≈ᵖ bern`; the biased coin `bernθ θ` (the indicator `[expansion r ≤ θ]`, `indB` for any decidable Borel set) has `E-at (bernθ θ) indT ≃ ι θ` for `θ ≤ 1`.
- `Below2-borel`, `flipS`, `coinKernel : Model AQ BoolQ`, `marg`, `E-at-marg`, `coinKernel²`, `E-at-kernel²`, `marg²`, `E-at-marg²`, `HH-from-H` (`QBS.Inference.CoinUniformPrior`) — the coin with a uniform prior on its bias: `coinKernel θ = bernθ θ` is a morphism (measurability in θ from the variable-threshold Borel set `Below2-borel`), `E-at (joinPre (mapPre coinKernel uniform)) indT ≃ ι ½` (P(heads) = ∫ θ dθ), and for two conditionally independent flips `E-at marg² (indT ⊗T indT) ≃ ι m` with `3m = 1` (P(HH) = ∫ θ² dθ), multiplied out `3·P(HH) = 2·P(H)`.
- `posterior`, `massP-rw`, `bayes`, `post1`, `post1-mean`, `E-at-post1-id³`, `post2-seq`, `post2-batch`, `post2-agree` (`QBS.Inference.CoinPosterior`) — Bayes' rule by reweighting and normalising: `posterior ℓ p n h = normPre (reweightPre ℓ (ιPre p)) n h` with `∫ f d(posterior) ≈ n · ∫ ℓ·f dp`; after one head under the uniform prior, `post1` is Beta(2,1) with `3 · E[θ | H] ≈ 2`; updating on H then H (`post2-seq`) is `≈ᵖ` updating on HH at once (`post2-batch`).
- `powT n`, `E-at-uniform-pow`, `nsum`, `moment-E`, `Fl`, `Kn`, `AH`, `E-at-Kn`, `margN`, `E-at-margN`, `heads-prob`, `HP`, `succession` (`QBS.Inference.CoinUniformPriorN`) — n flips of the coin with a uniform prior: `Kn n : AQ → T (Fl n)` is θ ↦ n+1 flips conditionally independent given θ (`nextK`, the recursion of `coinKernel²`), `E-at (lawAt (Kn n) θ) (AH n) ≃ θⁿ⁺¹`, `(n+1)·∫ uⁿ du ≃ 1` (`moment-E`, `nsum k x` the k-fold sum), `(n+2)·P(Hⁿ⁺¹) ≃ 1` (`heads-prob`) and Laplace's rule of succession multiplied out, `(n+2)·P(Hⁿ⁺¹) ≃ (n+1)·P(Hⁿ)` (`succession`, with `HP 0 = 1`).
- `bernθ-half≈bernB : bernθ ½ ≈ᵖ bernB` (`QBS.Inference.BernHalf`) — the biased coin at ½ is the fair coin of `BoolDist`.
- `joint`, `E-joint`, `riskX`, `risk-X : 6·r ≃ 1`, `riskP9`, `risk-P9`, `riskP`, `risk-P : 18·r ≃ 1`, `risk-X18 : 18·r(X) ≃ 3` (`QBS.Inference.CoinBayesRisk`) — the Bayes risk E[(θ̂ − θ)²] over the joint law of (θ, X) (one flip, uniform prior), the expectation of the expanded signed test θ̂² − 2θθ̂ + θ² built from `+T`, `scaleT`, `⊗T` and computed by `E-at-σ`, `E-at-kernel` and the uniform moments ∫θ = ½, ∫θ² = ⅓: the frequentist estimator θ̂ = X has r = ⅙ and the posterior mean θ̂ = (X+1)/3 has r = 1/18 (`riskP9` is 9r), so the posterior mean has the smaller Bayes risk; the arithmetic is in the abstract `Alg`, applied to an abstract copy of the signed ring.

### `QBS.Inference.Sampler`, `QBS.Inference.UniformSampler`
Statistical models as samplers (module parameter `lem₁ : LEM₁`; the `Sampler` module of `Inference/Sampler`).
- `Sampler Θ X` — a joint probe `Θ × ℛ ⇒ X`; `draw s θ`, `draw-random`, `single s g θ` — the bounded signed statistic of one draw on the fair coin; `obsAt`, `iidExperiment s g` — the iid experiment over the coordinates of the coin.
- `SingleMoments s g μ σ²` (`MeanAt`, `SecondAt`), `iid-moments`, `sampler-mean-unbiased` — the moments of one draw give those of the iid sample, so the unbiasedness theorems of `Inference/Frequentist` hold for every sampler.
- `Model Θ X`, `lawAt`, `samplerModel`, `E-at-sampler` — the kernel `Θ ⇒ TQ X` of a sampler; its `E-at` is the expectation of the single draw.
- `uniformSampler`, `idTest`, `singleMoments`, `moments`, `sample-mean-unbiased`, `sample-variance-unbiased` (`QBS.Inference.UniformSampler`) — validation: the uniform distribution as a sampler has the single-draw moments `E u`, `Var u`.

### `QBS.Inference.BernoulliCompare`
Bayesian vs frequentist on "is the coin fair or two-headed?" (module parameter `lem₁ : LEM₁`; `CoinBias` is used directly).
- `headSampler t : Sampler (1Q S) AQ` — the draw `[expansion r ≤ t]`; `twoHeadedS`, `fairS` (t = 1, ½), `sampler h`, `thr h`.
- `single-draw h`, `likelihood-is-expectation h` — `E-B fairCoin _ (single (sampler h) idTest _) ≃ ι (heads h)`: `E[head | two-headed] ≃ 1`, `E[head | fair] ≃ ½` (from `UniformCDF.cdf`).
- `evidence-is-prior-expectation` — `ι ½ *S E[head | two-headed] +S ι ½ *S E[head | fair] ≃ ι (½·1 + ½·½)`, the evidence of `CoinBias.evidence-heads` as the prior-weighted frequentist expectation.

### `QBS.Continuous.HalfLine`, `QBS.Continuous.HalfLineNegExp`, `QBS.Continuous.HalfLineUnit`, `QBS.Continuous.HalfLineNorm`, `QBS.Continuous.HalfLineMean`, `QBS.Continuous.HalfLineMoment2`, `QBS.Analysis.GeometricNorm`, `QBS.Analysis.GeometricMoment2`
Lebesgue measure on [0,∞) as an s-finite measure on the weight space `WQ = [0,∞]` (module parameter `lem₁ : LEM₁`).
- `shiftPt`, `shiftPre n`, `lebPre`, `Meas-shift` (`HalfLine`) — `shiftPre n` is the uniform on [0,1] shifted to `n` (probe `r ↦ (1, n + expansion r)` over the fair coin); `lebPre = sumPre shiftPre` is Lebesgue measure on [0,∞), `λ = ∑ₙ (n + U)`.
- `Meas-hshift`, `∫ᴹ-shift`, `∫ᴹ-leb` (`HalfLine`) — `∫ᴹ lebPre h ≈ ∑ᶠ n. J fairCoin (r ↦ h (n + expansion r))` for every morphism `h : WQ ⇒ WQ` (unbounded tests included).
- `negExpH`, `negExp-add`, `∫ᴹ-leb-negExp`, `∫ᴹ-leb-negExp-split`, `I-negExp` (`HalfLineNegExp`) — the test `x ↦ e^{−x}` as a morphism `WQ ⇒ WQ`; `e^{−(x+y)} ≈ e^{−x} e^{−y}` when `x`, `y`, `x+y` are bounded by naturals; `∫ e^{−x} dλ ≈ ∑ᶠ n. e^{−n} · I-negExp` with `I-negExp = ∫₀¹ e^{−u} du`.
- `G`, `F`, `q`, `F-split`, `xs-rec`, `I-negExp-unit` (`HalfLineUnit`) — `∫₀¹ e^{−u} du + e^{−1} ≈ 1` (no subtraction), by self-similarity: `F j = ∫₀¹ e^{−2⁻ʲ u} du` satisfies `F j ≈ ½ (e^{−2⁻⁽ʲ⁺¹⁾} F (j+1)) + ½ F (j+1)` (first-bit split and `negExp-add`).
- `negExp-pw`, `gb`, `norm-from`, `∫ᴹ-leb-negExp-norm` (`HalfLineNorm`) — `e^{−n} ≈ gⁿ`, and `∫ᴹ lebPre negExpH ≈ 1#`, i.e. `∫₀^∞ e^{−x} dx = 1`.
- `xNegExpH`, `KK`, `KK-split`, `ww-rec`, `K-pin`, `shift-split`, `∫ᴹ-leb-xNegExp` (`HalfLineMean`) — the mean `∫ᴹ lebPre xNegExpH ≈ 1#`, i.e. `∫₀^∞ x e^{−x} dx = 1`, for `xNegExpH : x ↦ x e^{−x}`.  With `K = ∫₀¹ u e^{−u} du`: `K-pin : K + (g + g) ≈ 1#` (self-similarity of `Kⱼ = ∫₀¹ u e^{−2⁻ʲu} du`, via `wⱼ = hⱼ²Kⱼ + hⱼqⱼ` and `GeometricNorm.norm-s`), `shift-split : ∫₀¹ (n+u) e^{−(n+u)} du ≈ e^{−n} (n I + K)`, then `GeometricNorm.GeoMean.total`.
- `x²NegExpH`, `LL`, `LL-split`, `x2-rec`, `L-pin`, `shift-split`, `∫ᴹ-leb-x²NegExp` (`HalfLineMoment2`) — the second moment `∫ᴹ lebPre x²NegExpH ≈ 1# + 1#`, i.e. `∫₀^∞ x² e^{−x} dx = 2`, for `x²NegExpH : x ↦ x² e^{−x}`.  With `L = ∫₀¹ u² e^{−u} du`: `L-pin : L + (((g + g) + (g + g)) + g) ≈ 1# + 1#` (self-similarity of `Lⱼ = ∫₀¹ u² e^{−2⁻ʲu} du`: the combination `x2 j = ½ hⱼ³ Lⱼ + (hⱼ + ½hⱼ²) qⱼ` has the recursion `x2-rec`, from the pins of `HalfLineUnit` and the mean at every scale, and `GeometricNorm.norm`), `shift-split : ∫₀¹ (n+u)² e^{−(n+u)} du ≈ e^{−n} (n² I + 2n K + L)`, then `GeoMom2.total`.
- `ss`, `two`, `U-inv`, `L-inv`, `base`, `norm`, `Geo.total` (`GeometricNorm`, `module GeometricNorm A R`, abstract in any `OmegaSemiring` with `Arch`) — `norm`: the recursion `x m ≈ (1 + q (m+1)) x (m+1)`, `q m ≈ q (m+1)²`, `q m ≤ 1`, the one-step facts (`base`) and `hp m q m ≤ x m ≤ hp m` give `x 0 + q 0 ≈ 1`, by error-doubling invariants and `arch`; `Geo.total`: `I + g ≈ 1` and `gᴺ (N+1) ≤ 1` give `(∑ᶠ gⁿ) I ≈ 1`.
- `Lsplit` (`abstract`, over `Lsplit′`, with the unfolding `Lsplit-def`), `x2-rec-alg`, `x2-lo`, `x2-hi`, `L5-alg`, `GeoMom2.total` (`GeometricMoment2`, `module GeometricMoment2 A R`, abstract) — the ring identities of the first-bit split of `L`, the recursion for `x2`, its bounds `h q ≤ x2 ≤ h`, and `GeoMom2.total`: `I + g ≈ 1`, `K + 2g ≈ 1`, `L + 5g ≈ 2` and `gᴺ (N+1) ≤ 1` give `∑ᶠ gⁿ (n² I + 2n K + L) ≈ 2` (the telescoping error `pol N gᴺ`, `pol N = N² + 2N + 2`, is small along `N = 4t`).

### `QBS.Continuous.SFiniteReweight`, `QBS.Continuous.ExpDist`, `QBS.Continuous.ExpDistSq`, `QBS.Inference.ExpDistMean`, `QBS.Inference.ExpDistVar`, `QBS.Inference.ExpDistPair`, `QBS.Inference.ProductMarginals`, `QBS.Continuous.DensityDist`, `QBS.Inference.DensityExpectation`

- `mulH`, `reweightPre`, `∫ᴹ-reweight` (`SFiniteReweight`) — reweighting `p : Pre (XW X)` by a density `d : X ⇒ WQ`: `∫ᴹ (reweightPre d p) f ≈ ∫ᴹ p (mulH d f)` with `mulH d f` the pointwise product.
- `expW`, `∫ᴹ-expW`, `massP-expW`, `expDist`, `∫-expDist`, `∫-expDist-one`, `idW`, `∫-expDist-id` (`ExpDist`) — the exponential distribution: `expW = reweightPre negExpH lebPre`, `massP expW ≈ 1#`, `expDist = normPre expW 1# _ : C.Pre WQ`, `∫ᴹ (ιPre expDist) f ≈ ∫ᴹ lebPre (mulH negExpH f)`, total mass `1#` and mean `∫ᴹ (ιPre expDist) idW ≈ 1#`.
- `idC`, `zeroC`, `idI`, `E-atᴵ-idI` (`ExpDistMean`) — `idI : ITest expDist` (`pos = id`, `neg = 0`) and `E-atᴵ expDist idI ≃ 1`, so `E-atᴵ-map`, `E-respᴵ` and linearity of `ExpectationInt` apply to the exponential distribution.
- `sqW`, `∫-expDist-sq` (`ExpDistSq`) — the second moment `∫ᴹ (ιPre expDist) sqW ≈ 1# + 1#` for the squaring statistic `sqW : x ↦ x·x`.
- `sqC`, `sqI`, `E-atᴵ-sqI`, `var-expDist` (`ExpDistVar`) — `sqI : ITest expDist` (`pos = x²`, `neg = 0`), `E-atᴵ expDist sqI ≃ 2`, and the variance `E-atᴵ expDist sqI +S (-S (E-atᴵ expDist idI *S E-atᴵ expDist idI)) ≃ 1` (`E[X²] − (E X)² = 1`; `Analysis/Variance.Var` needs a bounded function, so the difference is stated directly as a signed value).
- `densW`, `∫ᴹ-densW`, `massP-densW`, `densDist`, `∫-dens`, `∫-dens-one` — any normalised density on [0,∞) as a probability: for `d : Hom S WQ WQ` and `nrm : ∫ᴹ lebPre d ≈ 1#`, `densDist d nrm = normPre (reweightPre d lebPre) 1# _ : C.Pre WQ`, with `∫ᴹ (ιPre (densDist d nrm)) f ≈ ∫ᴹ lebPre (mulH d f)` for every `f : Hom S WQ WQ` and total mass `∫ᴹ (ιPre (densDist d nrm)) (constW 1#) ≈ 1#`.  Generalises `expDist` (`d = negExpH`).
- `toC`, `J-dens`, `densI`, `E-atᴵ-dens`, `zero-integral` — `densI d nrm g⁺ g⁻ b⁺ b⁻ : ITest (densDist d nrm)` for parts `g⁺ g⁻ : Hom S WQ WQ` whose integrals `∫ᴹ lebPre (mulH d g±)` are `Bnd`, and `E-atᴵ (densDist d nrm) (densI …) ≃ (∫ᴹ lebPre (mulH d g⁺) − ∫ᴹ lebPre (mulH d g⁻) [ b⁺ , b⁻ ])`; `zero-integral : ∫ᴹ lebPre (mulH d (constW 0#)) ≈ 0#`.
- `idD-integral`, `sqD-integral`, `idD`, `E-atᴵ-idD`, `sqD`, `E-atᴵ-sqD` — the exponential re-derived from `densDist negExpH` (`expNrm = ∫ᴹ-leb-negExp-norm`): the integrals `∫ᴹ lebPre (mulH negExpH idW) ≈ 1#` and `… sqW ≈ 1# + 1#` agree with `ExpDist`/`ExpDistSq`, and the mean and second moment are `E-atᴵ-dens` instances.
- `expPair`, `oneI`, `E-XY`, `E-X+Y`, `E-XYsq` (`ExpDistPair`) — two independent exponential draws `expPair = expDist ⊗ᵖ expDist`: `E[X·Y] ≃ 1`, `E[X + Y] ≃ 2`, and `E[X² + Y² + 2·X·Y] ≃ 6` (the second moment of `(X + Y)²`, written in its expanded form).
- `const-I`, `fst-marginal`, `snd-marginal` (`ProductMarginals`) — the marginal laws `mapPre fstQ (p ⊗ᵖ q) ≈ᵖ p` and `mapPre sndQ (p ⊗ᵖ q) ≈ᵖ q` for probability `Pre`s (tower law `∫-map`/`∫-join`/`∫-σ`; a probability measure integrates a constant to itself).
- `E-atᴵ-cong`, `fstI`, `sndI`, `E-fstI`, `E-sndI`, `sumSqI`, `E-atᴵ-sumSqI`, `var-sum` (`ProductMarginals`) — `E-atᴵ-cong`: integrable tests with pointwise-equal parts have `≃` expectations; `fstI`, `sndI : ITest expPair` (the coordinates, from `idI` by `transportI` along the marginal law and `precompI`) with expectation `1`; `sumSqI : ITest expPair` the pointwise statistic `(x , y) ↦ (x + y)²` (parts equal pointwise to those of `XYsq`), `E-atᴵ expPair sumSqI ≃ 6`, and the variance `E-atᴵ expPair sumSqI +S (-S (E[X + Y] *S E[X + Y])) ≃ 2`.

### `QBS.Analysis.TailFTC`, `QBS.Continuous.TailFTC`, `QBS.Continuous.TailFTCHalfLine`, `QBS.Continuous.TailFTCExp`, `QBS.Continuous.TailFTCExpHalf`

- `I-negExp-unit′`, `I-negExp-unit″` (`QBS.Continuous.TailFTCExp`) — `HalfLineUnit.I-negExp-unit` re-derived from `ftc-unit` with `h = G = e^{−·}`; `∫-negExp : ∫ᴹ lebPre negExpH ≈ 1#`, `tail : e^{−N} ≤ ½ᴺ` (`QBS.Continuous.TailFTCExpHalf`).

- `ftc-J` (`module TailFTCLipJ lem₁`, `module LipJ h k G s K c …`) — the same for integrands of any size, in the unbounded integral: the bounds `h x + c ≤ 2ˢ` etc. replace `≤ 1`, and the identity is `J fairCoin (h ∘ expansion) Mh + G 1# ≈ G 0# + J fairCoin (k ∘ expansion) Mk` (scaling by `½ˢ`, `J-scale`, `J-fun`).
- `glue`, `sum-ibp`, `tele-ibp` (`module TailFTCLipHalf lem₁`, `module Glue h k G unit G0 tl`) — integration by parts on `[0,∞)`: from the unit-interval identities `∫₀¹ h(n+u) + G(n+1) = G n + ∫₀¹ k(n+u)`, `G 0 = 0` and `G(N) → 0` along a subsequence (for every m and N₀ some N ≥ N₀ has `G (natE N) ≤ ½ᵐ`), `∫ᴹ lebPre h ≈ ∫ᴹ lebPre k`.

- `powH`, `pow-integral`, `powD`, `E-atᴵ-powD` (`module ExpMomentN lem₁`) — all moments of the exponential through the expectation API: `pow-integral n : ∫ᴹ lebPre (mulH negExpH (powH n)) ≈ fact n`, `powD n : ITest (densDist negExpH expNrm)` with parts `xⁿ` and `0`, and `E-atᴵ-powD n : E-atᴵ … (powD n) ≃ (fact n − 0# [ factB n , … ])`, i.e. `E[Xⁿ] ≃ n!`.

### `QBS.Inference.Domination`
- `Bnd-≤`, `Bnd-add`, `Bnd-scale`, `J-Bnd-mono`, `ITest-dom` (`module Domination lem₁`) — finiteness by domination: `Bnd` is downward closed (`a ≤ b → Bnd b → Bnd a`) and closed under sums and products; `J-Bnd-mono m p q : (∀ x → φ x ≤ ψ x) → Bnd (J m ψ q) → Bnd (J m φ p)`; `ITest-dom p t g⁺ g⁻` is an `ITest p` whose parts are bounded pointwise by those of `t`.
- `∫ᴹ-mono`, `addH`, `∫ᴹ-scale`, `∫ᴹ-add`, `∫ᴹ-dom`, `∫ᴹ-Bnd-dom` — the integral on `WQ` is monotone, scales (`∫ (c · f) ≈ c · ∫ f`) and adds; `g ≤ c · b` gives `∫ᴹ p g ≤ c · ∫ᴹ p b` and finiteness.
- `dens-dom`, `densB-dom`, `densI-dom`, `E-atᴵ-dom` — for a density `d`: `densI-dom d nrm g⁺ g⁻ b c cB bB dom⁺ dom⁻ : ITest (densDist d nrm)` from `g± x ≤ c · b x` and `Bnd (∫ᴹ lebPre (mulH d b))`, with `E-atᴵ-dom` the matching `E-atᴵ-dens`.

### `QBS.Inference.PolyDomination`
- `expI`, `onePlusPow`, `expB` (`module PolyDomination lem₁`) — `expI k c cB g⁺ g⁻ dom⁺ dom⁻ : ITest (densDist negExpH expNrm)` whenever `g± x ≤ c · (1 + xᵏ)`, from the moments `n!`.
- `HNDom`, `hnI`, `hnB`, `HNZ` — the same for the half-normal, with even growth `c · (1 + x^{ev m})`: `hnI g⁺ g⁻ m c cB dom⁺ dom⁻ : ITest halfNormal`; no bound on `n` is needed, since `n · (Z + (2m−1)‼ Z) ≈ 1 + (2m−1)‼`.
- `powD-dom`, `E-atᴵ-powD-dom`, `sqI-dom` — validation: `powD`, `E[Xⁿ] ≃ n!`, and the half-normal second-moment test re-derived by domination.
- `hn-alg` (`module AlgD`) — the algebra over an abstract ω-semiring.

### `QBS.Inference.PolyDominationOdd`
- `pw≤1+ev`, `hnIk`, `xI` (`module PolyDominationOdd lem₁`) — `pw≤1+ev k x : xᵏ ≤ 1 + x^{2k}` on `WQ` (split `x ≤ 1` or `1 ≤ x`), and `hnIk g⁺ g⁻ k c cB dom⁺ dom⁻ : ITest halfNormal` whenever `g± x ≤ c · (1 + xᵏ)` with `k` arbitrary (odd degrees included; the constant becomes `2c`).  `xI : ITest halfNormal` is the first moment.  The value `E[X] ≃ n` is not proved.

### `QBS.Inference.ExpRateOne`
- `expRate-one` (`module ExpRateOne lem₁`) — the rate-1 case of `expDistλ` (`Dist 1# 1# 0 0 …`) agrees with `expDist`: `∫ᴹ (ιPre expDistλ) f ≈ ∫ᴹ (ιPre expDist) f` for every test `f`.

### The real line and the Gaussian without π: `QBS.Continuous.RealLine`, `QBS.Continuous.RealDensity`, `QBS.Inference.SymExpectation`, `QBS.Inference.GaussReal`
- `RQ`, `inlH`, `inrH`, `copairH`, `lebR`, `∫ᴹ-lebR` (`module RealLine lem₁`) — the real line as the QBS coproduct `RQ = WQ +Q WQ` (`inlH x` is `+x`, `inrH x` is `−x`; zero appears twice, with measure zero), the copairing `copairH d e : RQ ⇒ WQ`, and Lebesgue measure `lebR : Pre (XW RQ)` as a `sumPre` of `mapM inlH lebPre`, `mapM inrH lebPre` and zero measures: `∫ᴹ-lebR f : ∫ᴹ lebR f ≈ ∫ᴹ lebPre (f ∘ inlH) + ∫ᴹ lebPre (f ∘ inrH)`.
- `∫ᴹ-lebR-dens`, `lebR-sym`, `toR` (`module RealDensity lem₁`) — `∫ᴹ lebR (mulH (copairH d e) f)` splits into the two half-line integrals; a symmetric density `copairH d d` with `∫ᴹ lebPre d ≈ c` has total mass `c + c`.
- `Sym`, `symDist`, `∫-sym`, `J-sym` (`module Sym d c hc n nn`) — the symmetric density normalised by any `n` with `(c + c) * n ≈ 1#`: `∫-sym f : ∫ᴹ (ιPre symDist) f ≈ n * (∫ᴹ lebPre (mulH d (f ∘ inlH)) + ∫ᴹ lebPre (mulH d (f ∘ inrH)))`.
- `E-atᴵ-≈`, `≃-≈`, `SymE`, `RH`, `symI`, `E-atᴵ-sym`, `RH-odd`, `E-atᴵ-odd` (`module SymExpectation lem₁`, `module SymE d c hc n nn`) — expectations under a symmetric density: `symI g⁺ g⁻ b⁺ b⁻ : ITest symDist`, `E-atᴵ-sym : E-atᴵ symDist (symI …) ≃ (RH g⁺ − RH g⁻ [ b⁺ , b⁻ ])`, and the symmetry lemma: an odd statistic (`g⁺ ∘ inlH ≈ g⁻ ∘ inrH`, `g⁺ ∘ inrH ≈ g⁻ ∘ inlH`) has `E-atᴵ-odd : E-atᴵ symDist (symI …) ≃ (0# − 0# [ … ])` for every symmetric density.
- `gaussR`, `xI`, `sqI`, `E-atᴵ-X`, `E-atᴵ-X²`, `var-gaussR` (`module GaussReal lem₁`) — the standard Gaussian on the whole real line, density `copairH phiH phiH` normalised by `n · ½` with `Z · n ≈ 1#` (`GaussInverse.Z-inv`), so `π` and the value of `Z` are never used: `E-atᴵ-X : E-atᴵ gaussR xI ≃ (0# − 0# [ … ])`, i.e. `E[X] ≃ 0`; `E-atᴵ-X² : E-atᴵ gaussR sqI ≃ (1# − 0# [ … ])`, i.e. `E[X²] ≃ 1`; and `var-gaussR : E[X²] +S -S (E[X] *S E[X]) ≃ 1`.  Integrability of `x` uses `x ≤ 1 + x²` by `Analysis/CutSplit.split1`.

- `lem1`, `lem2`, `cert1z`, `cert2z`, `pack1` (`module AlgGM A R`, `module Gauss1 E M m Mm E≤1 E-anti E-add E-b E-c`, `QBS.Analysis.GaussCertM1`) — the `j = −1` pack of `GaussCert`: with `G = Φ`, `h = x Φ` and `k = 0` (written `kz x = 0 · hh 0 x`) the certificate is first order, `Φ (a + w) + w (a Φ a) ≤ Φ a + w² 2ᶜ` and conversely, from `Y-up`, `Y-lo` and `Φ ≤ 1` alone; `pack1` bundles the hypotheses of `LipJ`.

- `absR`, `absI`, `E-atᴵ-abs`, `Z2-abs` (`module GaussRealAbs lem₁`) — the Gaussian absolute moment on the real line: `E-atᴵ gaussR absI ≃ ((n′ * (1# + 1#)) − 0# [ … ])` with `n′` the normaliser of `gaussR`, and `Z2-abs : (Z + Z) * (n′ * (1# + 1#)) ≈ 1# + 1#`, i.e. `(Z + Z) · E|X| ≃ 2`.

### Certificate calculus for the tail FTC: `QBS.Analysis.Deriv`, `QBS.Analysis.DerivProd`, `QBS.Analysis.DerivExp`, `QBS.Continuous.DerivBridge`, `QBS.Continuous.DerivMoment`, `QBS.Analysis.DerivChain`, `QBS.Analysis.DerivGauss`, `QBS.Continuous.DerivGaussMoment`
- `D`, `Deriv`, `Fin`, `Dmono`, `D-resp`, `Gup`, `Gdn`, `Dconst`, `Did`, `Dsum`, `Dscale`, `Dshift`, `Daff`, `DFconst`, `DFid`, `DFsum`, `DFscale`, `DFresp`, `DFshift`, `DFaff` (`module AlgD A R`, `QBS.Analysis.Deriv`) — `D M c G h k` says `G′ = k − h` on `[0, M]` with one constant `c`: congruence, bounds `≤ c`, Lipschitz `h`, `k` (`LpU`, `LpD`) and the two-sided certificate `G (a + w) + w h a ≤ G a + w k a + w² c` and its converse (`Ct1`, `Ct2`), at every point pair `a + w ≤ M`, `w ≤ 1`.  `Deriv M G h k` hides the constant (`Σ c (Fin c × D M c G h k)`, `Fin c` is `c ≤ 2ˢ`). - `Dprod`, `DFprod`, `Cp`, `Fin-Cp` (`module AlgDP A R`, `QBS.Analysis.DerivProd`) — the product rule `h = h₁G₂ + G₁h₂`, `k = k₁G₂ + G₁k₂`, constant `Cp c`, by multiplying the factors' certificates (no cancellation) and the Lipschitz bound of `G`.
- `pwD`, `DE`, `DFE`, `xjE` (`module AlgDE A R`, `module Expo`, `QBS.Analysis.DerivExp`) — `xⁱ` by the product rule, `(e^{−x})′ = −e^{−x}` from the exponential facts of `AlgP`, and `xjE fM j : Deriv M (xʲ⁺¹ e^{−x}) (xʲ⁺¹ e^{−x}) ((j+1) xʲ e^{−x})`.
- `Unit.ftc-unit`, `sum-ibp0`, `OnHalfLine.unit`, `OnHalfLine.glue-D`, `OnHalfLine.glue-D0` (`module DerivBridge lem₁`, `QBS.Continuous.DerivBridge`) — the bridge to `LipJ`: from `Deriv 1# G h k` and measurability, `J h + G 1 ≈ G 0 + J k`; from `Deriv (n+1) G h k` for every n, the identities on each `[n, n+1]` and `∫ h dλ ≈ ∫ k dλ` (`G 0 = 0`, `G(N) → 0` along a subsequence) or `∫ h dλ ≈ G 0 + ∫ k dλ` (any `G 0`).
- `dvn`, `moment-succ`, `∫-moment` (`module DerivMoment lem₁`, `QBS.Continuous.DerivMoment`) — validation: `∫ᴹ lebPre (pwNegExpH n) ≈ fact n` from the calculus, the same statement as `∫ᴹ-pw-moment`.
- `Elip`, `DX`, `Cx`, `Fin-Cx`, `DFexp` (`module AlgDX A R`, `module Core`, `module Chain`, `QBS.Analysis.DerivChain`) — the chain rule for the exponential: from `D M c u h k` (with `1 ≤ c`, `c + (c + c) ≤ B`) and the facts of `e^{−x}` at arguments `≤ B` (`E (x + y) = E x E y`, `E t + t E t ≤ 1 ≤ E t + t`, anti-monotone, `E ≤ 1`), `DX : D M (Cx c) (E ∘ u) (k · E ∘ u) (h · E ∘ u)`, i.e. `(e^{−u})′ = −e^{−u} u′`.  The two certificates use only additive increments (addition law on `x + Q` and `y + (P + e)`, the first-order facts, and the Lipschitz bound `Elip : q ≤ p + t → E p ≤ E q + t`); the errors are `w² (c² + c + (c + c)²)` and `w² (c + c (c + c))`.  `DFexp : Deriv M u h k → Deriv M (E ∘ u) (E(u) · k) (E(u) · h)` takes the exponential facts at every bound `P s`.
- `DFquad`, `Fin-half`, `DFgauss`, `DFgauss1` (`module AlgDG A R`, `QBS.Analysis.DerivGauss`) — `(½ x²)′ = x` (product rule, scalar `½`); `Φ = e^{−x²/2}`: `Deriv M Φ (x Φ) 0` and, by the product rule on `x · Φ`, `Deriv M (x Φ) (x² Φ) (x⁰ Φ)`, in the `pw` form of the tests `gMomH`.
- `Ea`, `Eb`, `Ec`, `dv1`, `dv2`, `Φtail`, `∫-xφ`, `∫-x²φ` (`module DerivGaussMoment lem₁`, `QBS.Continuous.DerivGaussMoment`) — validation through `glue-D0`: `∫₀^∞ x e^{−x²/2} ≈ 1` and `∫₀^∞ x² e^{−x²/2} ≈ Z` (the statements of `∫-xφ` and `∫-x²φ` in the hand-written Gauss modules), with `Z` as in `GaussMoment`.

## Signed layer, Gaussians and information quantities

The newest part of the library (`plans/03-bayesian-frequentist.md`). A *signed test* is a function `f` written as two
non-negative parts, `f⁺ − f⁻`, each with values in `[0, ∞]`. This lets us take expectations of functions that are not
bounded, such as `x`, `x²` or `log x`. Every module below takes the parameter `lem₁ : LEM₁` (see `Classical.md`).

### `QBS.Continuous.SignedTest`
- `SgT` — `SgT X`: a signed test on `X`, a pair of tests `X ⇒ WQ` (`WQ` is `[0, ∞]`) with parts `posT`, `negT` and pointwise values `at⁺`, `at⁻`.
- `_≐_`, `≐-refl`, `≐-sym`, `≐-trans` — equality of signed tests, `f⁺ + g⁻ ≈ g⁺ + f⁻` pointwise; transitive through a pointwise finite middle.
- `_+T_`, `scaleT`, `_*T_`, `constT`, `fromW`, `_∘T_` — pointwise sum, scaling by a signed constant, product, constants, a non-negative test as a signed one, precomposition.
- `idT`, `sqT` — `x⁺ − x⁻` and its square, on the real line.
- `toW`, `toCut` — the identity bridge between tests into `CutQ` and into `WQ` (the same carrier and random elements).

### `QBS.Continuous.SignedExpect`
- `Int` — `Int p f`: both parts of `f` have finite integrals against `p`; `Int⁻` — only the negative part does.
- `Ex` — `Ex p f i = ∫ f⁺ − ∫ f⁻`, a signed value; `Ex-irr` says it does not depend on the proof `i`.
- `Ex-cong`, `Ex-+`, `Ex-neg`, `Ex-scale`, `Ex-const`, `Ex-mono` — respects `≐`, linearity, constants against a probability, monotonicity.
- `Int-dom` — integrability by domination; `Int-+`, `Int-neg`, `Int-scale`, `Int-const` — closure properties.
- `Ex∞`, `Ex∞-ι`, `Ex∞-+`, `Ex∞-mono` — the one-sided expectation in `(−∞, ∞]` when only `∫ f⁻` is finite; it agrees with `Ex` when both parts are finite.

### `QBS.Continuous.SignedInf`
- `Sg∞` — signed values in `(−∞, ∞]`: `p − n` with `n` finite and `p` any element of `[0, ∞]`; `_≃∞_` is equality, `_≤∞_` the order.
- `_+∞_`, `scale∞`, `top∞`, `fromPos` — total addition (there is no `−∞`, so no `∞ − ∞`), scaling, `+∞`, a value of `[0, ∞]` as a signed one.
- `ι∞`, `fin∞` — `Sg → Sg∞`, and back for values with finite positive part.
- `≃∞-trans`, `≤∞-trans`, `≤∞-antisym`, `+∞-comm`, `+∞-assoc`, `+∞-mono` — laws (transitivity uses excluded middle on whether a part is `∞`).

### `QBS.Continuous.SignedTransport`
- `Ex-map`, `Int-map`, `Int-unmap` — the expectation against a pushforward `mapM h p` is the expectation of `f ∘ h`.
- `Ex-scalePre`, `Int-scalePre` — against `c · p` (`c` finite) the expectation is `c` times the expectation.
- `ofITest`, `Ex-ofITest`, `toITest`, `E-atᴵ-toITest` — the bridge to the probability-monad API: an integrable test `ITest P` gives a signed test with `E-atᴵ P t ≃ Ex …`, and back.

### `QBS.Continuous.SignedLimits`
- `SgConv` — `SgConv a l`: signed values `a n` converge to `l` (tolerance `½ᵏ`, cross-added); `SgConv-cong`, `SgConv-+`, `SgConv-neg`, `SgConv-scale` — its laws.
- `dct-Ex` — dominated convergence for `Ex`.
- `deriv-Ex` — differentiation under the integral along steps `½ⁿ`, stated without division.

### `QBS.Continuous.SignedFubini`
- `innerT`, `Int-inner`, `Ex-fubini` — Fubini for signed tests: `Ex (s ⊗ t) f ≃ Ex t (innerT s f)` for `f` integrable against the product.
- `_⊗T_`, `Int-⊗`, `Ex-⊗` — the product `f(x) g(y)` of signed tests and independence: `Ex (s ⊗ t) (f ⊗ g) ≃ Ex s f · Ex t g`.

### `QBS.Continuous.SignedLog`
- `logP`, `logN` — the parts of the logarithm of a lower real, `log⁺ x = log (x ⊔ 1)` and `log⁻ x = log (1 / (x ⊓ 1))`, both in `[0, ∞]`.
- `logP-ge`, `logP-le`, `logN-le`, `logN-ge` — `e^{logP x} ≈ x` for `1 ≤ x < ∞`, `logP x ≈ 0` for `x ≤ 1`, and the same for `logN`.
- `logS`, `log-mul`, `log-one`, `log-exp` — `log x` as a signed value for `0 < x < ∞`; `log (x y) ≃ log x + log y`.
- `log∞`, `nlog∞` — `log x` for `x > 0` and `−log x` for `x < ∞` as values in `(−∞, ∞]`.
- `Meas-logP`, `Meas-logN` — the parts are measurable, so they can serve as test parts.

### `QBS.Continuous.SignedVar`
- `Var` — `Var P f i = E[f²] − (E f)²` as a signed value, for `f` with `f` and `f²` integrable (`Int₂`).
- `cen`, `Ex-cen`, `Var-cen` — the centred test `f − c`; `E[(f − c)²] ≃ E[f²] − 2c E f + c²`; with `c = E f` this is the variance.
- `Var-shift`, `Var-scale`, `Var-cong` — `Var (f + c) ≃ Var f`, `Var (c f) ≃ c² Var f`, respects `≐`.

### Gaussians: `QBS.Inference.GaussScale`, `QBS.Inference.GaussShift`, `QBS.Inference.GaussVar`
The standard Gaussian `gaussR` is in `QBS.Inference.GaussReal` (above); no value for `π` is ever used. Below, `s l ≈ 1` and `μ` is finite.
- `gaussσ`, `push-sym`, `second` — N(0, s²) as the pushforward of `gaussR` along `x ↦ s x`; `second : ∫ x² dN(0, s²) ≈ s²` (`GaussScale`).
- `gaussμ`, `gaussμ⁻`, `∫-gaussμ`, `mean`, `mean⁻` — N(±μ, s²) by translation, and its mean without subtraction: `E[X⁺] ≈ μ + E[X⁻]` (`GaussShift`).
- `cvar`, `cvar⁻` — the centred second moment `∫ (x − μ)² dN(μ, s²) ≈ s²` (`GaussVar`).
- `Ex-id`, `Var-gaussμ`, `Ex-id⁻`, `Var-gaussμ⁻` — the signed mean `E X ≃ ±μ` and variance `Var X ≃ s²` of the identity test under N(±μ, s²) (`GaussVar`).

### Information quantities: `QBS.Continuous.Entropy`, `QBS.Continuous.KLDiv`, `QBS.Continuous.InfoProduct`
For a base measure `μ` and a density `p : X ⇒ WQ`, the measure `p · μ` is `densPre μ p`.
- `logT`, `nlogT`, `densPre`, `∫-dens` — the signed tests `log p` and `−log p`, and the measure with density `p` (`Entropy`).
- `EntInt`, `Ent` — `Ent μ p i = E_{p·μ}[−log p] ∈ (−∞, ∞]`, defined when `∫ p log⁺ p dμ` is finite (`Entropy`).
- `Ent-le1`, `ent`, `ent-const` — for densities at most 1 (counting measure) the entropy is `∫ p log⁻ p dμ`; a constant density `c` gives `c log (1/c)` times the mass (`Entropy`).
- `KLInt`, `KL`, `KLInt-fin` — `KL Q p i = ∫ p log p dQ ∈ (−∞, ∞]`, defined when `∫ p log⁻ p dQ` is finite, which holds whenever `Q` has finite mass (`KLDiv`).
- `KL-nonneg` — Gibbs' inequality: `KL ≥ 0` when `Q` and `p · Q` are probability measures (`KLDiv`).
- `KL-one`, `KL-cong` — the density 1 has divergence 0; equal densities have equal divergence (`KLDiv`).
- `KL-⊗`, `Ent-⊗`, `KLInt-⊗`, `EntInt-⊗` — KL divergence and entropy add over independent products of normalised, positive, finite densities (`InfoProduct`).

## Bayesian and frequentist inference

### `QBS.Inference.Bayesian`
Generic in a `NormalizableSemiring`; there is no division, so normalisation goes through an inverse of the evidence.
- `sequential`, `evidence-sequential`, `posterior-seq` — observing `x₁` then `x₂` is observing both, with the product likelihood.
- `predictive-chain` — the chain rule for the evidence and the posterior predictive.
- `likSeq`, `likSeq-counts`, `posterior-exchangeable` — the Bernoulli model: the likelihood of a sequence depends only on the counts of successes and failures.
- `evidence-total`, `evidence-total-prob` — the total evidence over a discrete set of parameters.

### `QBS.Inference.CoinBias`
- `heads`, `twoHeaded`, `fair`, `evidence-heads`, `mass-twoHeaded`, `mass-fair` — is the coin fair or two-headed? One head gives evidence ¾ and posterior odds 2 : 1.

### `QBS.Inference.Frequentist`, `QBS.Inference.UniformExperiment`
Probabilities are over the data, for a fixed parameter.
- `eval`, `bias`, `variance`, `mse`, `Unbiased`, `Moments` — a statistic is a ring term in the observations (`var`, `nat`, `_+ₜ_`, `_*ₜ_`), so sample mean and variance are statistics; `bias` and the rest are computed in the ring of bounded random variables (`Frequentist`).
- `sample-mean-unbiased`, `sample-variance-unbiased` — the estimator theorems for any experiment with the right moments.
- `uniformExp` — the instance: independent draws uniform on `[a, a + c]`; `Sₙ / n` is unbiased for `a + c/2` (`UniformExperiment`).

### `QBS.Analysis.ExpRate`, `QBS.Analysis.ExpRateExt`, `QBS.Analysis.ExpRateSigned`, `QBS.Continuous.HalfLineRate`, `QBS.Continuous.ExpRateInt`, `QBS.Continuous.ExpRateMoment`, `QBS.Inference.ExpRateDist`
Exponential distribution with a rate λ.  The rate is an element `l` of the cuts with a reciprocal `c` (`l * c ≈ 1#`, since the cuts have no division), `l ≤ natE (suc μ)` and `c ≤ natE (suc ν)`.  Every positive integer or rational `(a+1)/(b+1)`, with no relation between `a` and `b`, is an instance (`Rat`).  The rate-1 data (`Ex`) is stated for widths `w ≤ 1` and is moved to `x ↦ λx` with width `λ w`; that width is at most 1 only for `w ≤ ½ᴷ` with `2ᴷ ≥ λ`, and `AlgExt` passes the Lipschitz bounds and the certificates from those widths to all widths by halving.
- `cert0`, `rs`, `u-eq` (`module AlgRate A R`, `QBS.Analysis.ExpRate`) — pointwise algebra over an abstract Archimedean ω-semiring.  `cert0` is the plain certificate for `G = c e^{−λx}`, `h = e^{−λx}` on a piece of width `w` (from `y + λw y ≤ 1 ≤ y + λw` and `c λ = 1`); `rs` multiplies a rate-1 certificate at `(λa, λw)` by `s c`, using `c λ = 1`.
- `Fn`, `Rate`, `Tl`, `Sc` (`module AlgRate A R`) — `Fn` is the scaled data `hR = s H(λx)`, `kR = s (j+1) K(λx)`, `GR = s c H(λx)` for the rate-1 functions `H = xʲ⁺¹ e^{−x}`, `K = xʲ e^{−x}`; `Rate` proves the Lipschitz bounds and both certificates with one constant `2^W` from the rate-1 `Ex.pack`; `Tl` is the tail `κ xⁱ e^{−λN} ≤ ½ᵐ` along `N = ν N′` from the rate-1 tail; `Sc` holds the identifications with `xⁿ e^{−λx}`, `pw-lc` (`cⁿ λⁿ ≈ 1`), `rate-eq`, and `var-eq`.
- `Plain`, `Lip`, `Cert2`, `up-ext`, `dn-ext`, `ext1`, `ext2`, `alg1`, `alg2` (`module AlgExt A R`, `QBS.Analysis.ExpRateExt`) — from small to all widths over an abstract Archimedean ω-semiring: a plain certificate `G (a+t) + t h(a+t) ≤ G a ≤ G (a+t) + t h a` (for antitone `h`), a Lipschitz bound `u (a+t) ≤ u a + t E`, and the certificates up to `t² E`, valid for `t ≤ ½ᴷ` pass to every `t ≤ 1` by halving the piece `K` times (`alg1`, `alg2` are the algebra of one step: the errors `4 (½t)² E` add up to `t² E`).
- `var-val` (`module AlgSigned A R l c lc`, `QBS.Analysis.ExpRateSigned`) — the signed arithmetic of the variance, over the abstract semiring.
- `negExpRH`, `pwRH`, `qH`, `scaleH`, `∫-scaleH` (`module HalfLineRate lem₁`) — the tests `x ↦ e^{−λx}`, `x ↦ xⁱ e^{−λx}`, `x ↦ (λx)ⁱ e^{−λx}` and `x ↦ κ f x`, with `∫ᴹ lebPre (scaleH κ f) ≈ κ * ∫ᴹ lebPre f`.
- `∫-negExpR`, `∫-negExpR-rate` (`module ExpRateInt lem₁`, `module Int l c μ ν lc lμ cν`) — `∫ᴹ lebPre (negExpRH l) ≈ c` and `l * ∫ᴹ lebPre (negExpRH l) ≈ 1#`, from the tail FTC with `G = c e^{−λx}`.
- `moment`, `moment-succ`, `mom0` (`module ExpRateMoment lem₁`, `module Mom l c μ ν lc lμ cν`) — `∫ᴹ lebPre (pwRH l n) ≈ pw (suc n) c * fact n`, i.e. `λⁿ⁺¹ ∫ xⁿ e^{−λx} = n!`; the step `∫ xʲ⁺¹ e^{−λx} = c (j+1) ∫ xʲ e^{−λx}` is `glue` with `h = cʲ⁺¹ H(λx)`, `k = cʲ⁺¹ (j+1) K(λx)`, `G = cʲ⁺² H(λx)`.  `Rat a b` gives `ratL`, `ratC`, `ratLC`, `ratLμ`, `ratCν` for the rate `(a+1)/(b+1)`.
- `expNrmλ`, `expDistλ`, `powλD`, `E-atᴵ-powλD`, `rate-moment`, `mean-rate`, `second-rate`, `var-rate`, `DistRat` (`module ExpRateDist lem₁`, `module Dist l c μ ν lc lμ cν`) — the density `λ e^{−λx}` is normalised, `expDistλ = densDist d expNrmλ`, `E-atᴵ-powλD n : E[Xⁿ] ≃ (pw n c * fact n − 0# [ … ])`, and the rescaled statements `E[Xⁿ] · λⁿ ≃ n!` (`rate-moment`), `E[X] · λ ≃ 1`, `E[X²] · λ² ≃ 2` and `(E[X²] − (E X)²) · λ² ≃ 1`.  `DistRat a b` is `Dist` at the rate `(a+1)/(b+1)`, for all `a` and `b` (`DistRat a 0` is the positive integer `a+1`).

### `QBS.Inference.PushforwardExpectation`, `QBS.Analysis.AffineAlg`, `QBS.Inference.ExpPushforward`, `QBS.Inference.GaussAffine`
Families as pushforwards (item B of the plan): a random variable is a map, `E_{f∗p}[g] = E_p[g ∘ f]`, so a parameterised family needs no new integral.
- `pushI`, `E-atᴵ-push`, `constI`, `E-atᴵ-const` (`module PushforwardExpectation lem₁`) — `pushI f p c g⁺ g⁻ ep en : ITest (mapPre f p)` from an integrable test `c` of `p` whose parts agree pointwise with the composites `g± ∘ f`, and `E-atᴵ-push : E-atᴵ (mapPre f p) (pushI …) ≃ E-atᴵ p c`; `constI p a` is the constant test with `E-atᴵ-const : E-atᴵ p (constI p a) ≃ a`.  The measure-level statement `∫ᴹ (mapM f μ) g ≈ ∫ᴹ μ (g ∘ f)` is `∫ᴹ-map`.
- `ι-cong`, `var-core`, `moment-two`, `scale-ι`, `mean-aff`, `var-aff` (`module AlgAff A`, `QBS.Analysis.AffineAlg`) — the signed algebra, over an abstract ω-semiring, so the solver never sees concrete cuts: `var-core` gives `E₂ − E₁² ≃ s` from `E₁ ≃ a`, `E₂ ≃ d`, `d ≈ s + a·a`; `mean-aff` and `var-aff` give `E[m + sX] ≃ m` and `E[Y²] − (E Y)² ≃ s²` from `E X ≃ 0`, `E X² ≃ 1`.
- `expPush`, `powPushD`, `E-atᴵ-powPush`, `mean-push`, `second-push`, `var-push`, `Rate` (`module ExpPushforward lem₁`, `module Push c cB`, `module Rate l lc lB`) — Exp with scale `c` is `mapPre (x ↦ c·x)` of Exp(1), for every finite `c`: `E-atᴵ-powPush n : E[Xⁿ] ≃ cⁿ n!` (from `∫ e^{−x}(c·x)ⁿ = cⁿ n!`), the mean `c`, the second moment `2c²` and `var-push : E[X²] − (E X)² ≃ c²`; for a rate `l` with `l c ≈ 1`, `mean-rate : E[X]·λ ≃ 1` and `var-rate : Var·λ² ≃ 1`, as in `ExpRateDist`.
- `affI`, `E-atᴵ-affI`, `aff2I`, `var-affI` (`module GaussAffine lem₁`) — N(μ, σ²) from `gaussR` by the statistic `μ + σX` for signed `μ`, `σ` (`RQ` has no addition): `E-atᴵ-affI m s : E[μ + σX] ≃ μ` and `var-affI m s : E[Y²] − (E Y)² ≃ σ²`, with `Y² = (μ² + 2μσ X) + σ² X²` built by linearity.

### `QBS.Analysis.DyadicAlg`, `QBS.Continuous.FirstBitInf`, `QBS.Continuous.HalfLineScale`, `QBS.Inference.ExpPushforwardAgree`
Dyadic scaling of Lebesgue measure on the half-line (first step of the change of variables, item B of the plan).
- `⨆-mix`, `mix-scale`, `dbl`, `ix`, `natE-dbl`, `cons-alg`, `∑ᶠ-dbl`, `unhalf`, `mulswap`, `tw`, `tw-hp` (`module AlgDy A half hh`, `QBS.Analysis.DyadicAlg`) — the algebra over an abstract ω-semiring: the supremum of mixtures is the mixture of suprema; `dbl n = 2n`, `ix false n = 2n`, `ix true n = 2n + 1`; `cons-alg b n (e ≈ [b]·½ + ½w) : 2(n + e) ≈ natE (ix b n) + w`; the reindexing `∑ᶠ-dbl : ∑ᶠ a ≈ ∑ᶠ n. (a (2n) + a (2n+1))` over ℕ × Bool ≅ ℕ; `tw k = 2ᵏ` with `tw k · ½ᵏ ≈ 1`.
- `firstBit-J` (`module FirstBitInf lem₁`) — the first-bit split of the fair coin for every `Meas φ`, unbounded: `J fairCoin φ ≈ ½ J fairCoin (φ ∘ consR true) + ½ J fairCoin (φ ∘ consR false)` (the bounded `FirstBit.firstBit-fun` on each truncation, then `⨆-mix`).
- `dblH`, `∫-dbl`, `∫-half`, `∫-tw`, `∫-tw′` (`module HalfLineScale lem₁`) — for every test `h : WQ ⇒ WQ`: `∫ lebPre (h ∘ dblH) ≈ ½ · ∫ lebPre h` (`dblH = scaleH 2 idW`, `x ↦ 2x`), `∫ lebPre (h ∘ scaleH ½ idW) ≈ 2 · ∫ lebPre h`, `∫ lebPre (h ∘ scaleH (tw k) idW) ≈ ½ᵏ ∫ lebPre h`, and `tw k · ∫ lebPre (h ∘ scaleH (tw k) idW) ≈ ∫ lebPre h`; proved by `∫ᴹ-leb`, the first-bit split of each unit interval and the reindexing `∑ᶠ-dbl`.
- `dens2`, `nrm2`, `agree` (`module ExpPushforwardAgree lem₁`) — validation: `dens2` is the density `2e^{−2x}`, `nrm2 : ∫ dens2 ≈ 1` from `∫-dbl`, and `agree f : ∫ᴹ (ιPre expPush½) f ≈ ∫ᴹ (ιPre (densDist dens2 nrm2)) f` for every test `f`, the pushforward Exp(½) of `ExpPushforward` having the same integrals as the density distribution.
