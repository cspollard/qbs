# Public interface

The names a user imports, with the module they live in and one line of meaning. Types are shown where short; open the
module for the rest. `scripts/doccheck.py` verifies that every module path below exists and every name still
occurs in it. Conventions: `ℓ` universe level, `S` a `RandomSource`, `Hom S X Y` morphisms in `QBSCat S`.

## Abstract layer

### `QBS.Core`
- `idH` — `idH S {X}`: the identity morphism (`S` explicit).
- `RandomSource` — a one-object site: `Measurability` (a setoid `Rˢ` with carrier `R` and equality `≈ᴿ`, and `Meas`, a monoid of setoid maps `Func Rˢ Rˢ` closed under `≈` and containing the constants), `Partitions` (`Idx`, the probes of discrete `ℕˢ`, along which `Meas` glues: `meas-glue`) and `NatPairing` (`pair`/`unpair`) plus `idx-glue` (covers compose); their fields are re-exported.
- `Probes` — `Probes M X`: a predicate `Random` on setoid maps `Func Rˢ X`, closed under `≈` (`random-resp`), constants and precomposition by `Meas`; `measProbes` is `Meas` itself (the representable); `random-glue-∘` says gluing commutes with postcomposition by a setoid map, and `random-glue-pre` that gluing along `j` then precomposing with `γ` is gluing the precomposed family along `j ∘ γ`.
- `QBS` — a quasi-Borel space: a setoid `Carrier` with `probes` that glue along partitions (`glue-random`, the sheaf condition). The core never assumes `≡` on `R`: each source chooses its equality.
- `CodiscQ` — `CodiscQ S A`: the codiscrete QBS on a setoid (every sequence random); the terminal object, the weights object and `Disc` of the examples are instances.
- `GluedQBS` — the QBS generated by a presheaf of pieces (pieces `P`, `ev`, `pre`, `ev-pre`, constant pieces): random iff locally a piece; `_+Q_`, `CoeqQ`, `ΣQ` and `DiscQ` are instances.
- `Hom` — a setoid map `map` preserving random elements (`map-random`).
- `glueₛ`, `constₛ`, `⇑`, `_∘ₛ_`, `ℕˢ` — gluing a family of setoid maps along an index map, constant maps, any function out of a `≡`-setoid, composition, discrete ℕ. `refineₛ j i` (in `NatPairing`) is the composite cover of `j` by the `i n`.

### `QBS.Category`, `QBS.Limits`, `QBS.Colimits`, `QBS.Complete`, `QBS.Exponential`
- `QBSCat` — `QBSCat S`, the category (agda-categories `Category`); `QBS.Category.Forget`: `Forget` and `Forget-faithful`, the faithful forgetful functor to `Setoids`; `QBS.Category.Concrete`: `QBSSub` (the `SubCategory` of `Setoids` of probe-preserving maps) with the strong equivalence `QBS≃Sub`, the codiscrete and discrete QBS `CodiscQ`/`DiscQ`, the adjoint triple `Disc⊣Forget`, `Forget⊣Codisc`, and the preservation theorems `Forget-continuous`, `Forget-cocontinuous`, `Codisc-continuous`, `Disc-cocontinuous`; `QBS.Category.Cartesian`: `cartesian`, `V`, `braided`, the cartesian, cartesian-monoidal and braided structures of `QBSCat S` (module parameter `S`); `QBS.Category.Measurable`: `MeasCat` (measurable maps as a one-object `SubCategory`) and `yoneda : MeasCat → QBSCat S` (object ↦ `ℛQ`), full and faithful, with `Forget∘yoneda≡Sub`.
- `QBS.Exponential`: `cartesianClosed` — `CartesianClosed (QBSCat S)`; `QBS.Limits`: terminal object and products; `QBS.Colimits`: coproducts.
- `QBS.Complete`: `countableProduct`, `countableCoproduct` — indexed products and coproducts.

### `QBS.Probes`
- `ℛQ` — the probe object, the space of random sources; random elements of `X` correspond to morphisms `ℛQ ⇒ X`.

### `QBS.Probability`
- `ProbabilityInterface` — an abstract commutative strong monad of probability measures over a cartesian closed category with probe object `ℛ`, assembled from the monad (`ProbMonad`), `ProbLaws` and `Fubini` (fields re-exported).
- `Measure` — the type of probability measures on `ℛ`; `measure μ : ⊤ ⇒ T ℛ` embeds a measure as a global element of the monad, and `law a μ = T a ∘ measure μ : ⊤ ⇒ T X` (derived) is the distribution of a probe `a : ℛ ⇒ X` under `μ`.
- `measure-push` (pushforward is `T m`), `measure-total` (mass one), `law-surjective` — the axioms linking measures to the monad; `law-resp`, `law-map`, `law-pre`, `law-return` are derived from them; `split`, `tensor`, `measure-tensor` (`T split.from ∘ measure (tensor μ ν) ≈ ψ ∘ ⟨measure μ, measure ν⟩`, with `ψ` the monoidal structure of the commutative monad) — the Fubini axiom; `kernel`, `kernel-law`, `law-join` are derived from it.

### `QBS.Statistical`
`StatisticalInterface` on top of a `ProbabilityInterface`, assembled from five small structures (their fields are re-exported, so `open StatisticalInterface St` still gives every name), plus `scaled-laws` (every measure is a countable sum of scaled laws):
- `CommutativeMonoidWithZero` (`weights`): `W`, `mulW`, `oneW`, `zero⇒`, and `mul-assoc`, `mul-comm`, `mul-one`, `mul-zero`.
- `SubMonad` (`measures`): `commutativeMonad` (the measure monad `M`), `inclusion` (Prob into `M`), `inclusion-strong`.
- `Score` (`scoring`): `zero` (zero measure), `score : W ⇒ M 1`, `score-zero`, `score-one`, `score-mul`, `zero-bind`.  It *defines* `scaleM a t = μ ∘ M t ∘ score ∘ a` and proves `scale-one`, `scale-mul`, `scale-return`, `scale-nat`, `scale-join`.
- `CountableSums` (`sums`): `sumK` (sums of kernels), `sumK-point`, `bind-sumK`, `sum-nat`, `sum-join`; `sumM` is `sumK` on global measures.  `scale-sum` is derived from `bind-sumK`.
- `Normalization` (`normalization`): `normalize : ∀ X → M X ⇒ Prob X + 1`, `normalize-prob`, `normalize-zero`, `normalize-scale`.

### `QBS.StatisticalLemmas`
- `bind-sum`, `bind-scale` — bind preserves countable sums and scaling of the measure argument.

### `QBS.IsQBSLike`, `QBS.IsStatisticalQBSLike`, `QBS.Instance`
- `IsQBSLike`, `IsStatisticalQBSLike` — the minimal structure for probability, resp. soft conditioning.
- `isQBSLike S P`, `isStatisticalQBSLike S P St` — package any random source with a (hypothesised) monad.

## Discrete model

### `QBS.Model.Semiring`
- `CompleteSemiring` — a commutative semiring with countable sums `∑` (fields `_+_`, `_*_`, `0#`, `1#`, `∑`).
- `NormalizableSemiring` — a complete semiring with inverses for normalisation (`complete` is the underlying semiring).

### `QBS.Model.Monad`
- `Measures` — `module Measures A K`: measures over `A` of weight class `K`.
- `Pre` — a discrete pre-measure: probe `α`, weights `μ`, proof `μ-ok`; `mk` builds one.
- `∫` — `∫ p f`: the expectation `∑ r. μ r * f (α r)`.
- `ηPre`, `mapPre`, `joinPre`, `∫-η`, `∫-join` — unit, map, join and their integral rules.

### `QBS.Model.WeightClasses`
- `probClass`, `allClass` — total mass 1 / arbitrary weights; `QBS.Model.SubProb`: `subClass` — total mass ≤ 1.

### `QBS.Model.Discrete`, `QBS.Model.StatInstance`
- `discreteStatisticalQBSLike` — `NormalizableSemiring → IsStatisticalQBSLike`, the discrete model.
- `discreteStat` (`StatInstance`) — its `StatisticalInterface`, with `normalize`.

### Concrete semirings: `QBS.Model.BoolSemiring`, `QBS.Model.ViterbiSemiring`, `QBS.Model.AdditiveSemiring`, `QBS.Model.AdditiveModel`, `QBS.Model.BoolModel`, `QBS.Model.ViterbiModel`
- `boolComplete`, `additiveComplete`, `additiveNormalizable` — complete / normalisable semirings (Boolean, additive `[0,∞]`); models `QBS.Model.BoolModel`, `ViterbiModel`, `AdditiveModel` (`additiveStatisticalQBSLike`).

## Continuous model

### `QBS.Borel.Source`
- `cantorSource` — Cantor space `2^ℕ` as a `RandomSource`.
- `measₛ`, `idxₛ` — a measurable map or a partition as a setoid map (Borel sets respect pointwise equality).

### `QBS.Continuous.Sample`, `QBS.Analysis.CoinIntegral`, `QBS.Analysis.Omega`
- `UnitInterval` (`Sample`) — `module UnitInterval A`: the unit interval as a QBS `AQ`; `S` — the Cantor random source used by the continuous model.
- `lemOf` (`CoinIntegral`) — turns `lem₁` into the `lem` the lower-cut construction needs; `LowerRealsOmega (lemOf lem₁)` is the value semiring.
- `OmegaSemiring` (`Omega`) — the value-semiring record (`_≈_`, `1#`, order, suprema).

### `QBS.Analysis.Measure`, `QBS.Analysis.Fun`
- `Integration` (`Measure`) — `module Integration A`: `probClass` (probability measures of the continuous model; not the discrete `WeightClasses.probClass`).
- `Measure` — a sub-probability measure as a Daniell functional on measurable functions; `IsProb` — total mass 1.
- `Fun` — the inductive class of measurable `[0,1]`-valued functions on Cantor space.

### `QBS.Analysis.CoinIntegral`, `QBS.Continuous.FairCoin`
- `fairCoin` — the fair-coin measure on Cantor space; `fairCoin-cyl` — its value on cylinders.

### `QBS.Analysis.Meas`, `QBS.Analysis.Lebesgue`
- `Meas` — threshold measurability of `[0,∞]`-valued functions; `Lebesgue`: `J m φ` — the integral of a `Meas` function (unbounded).

### `QBS.Analysis.Cauchy`, `QBS.Analysis.Exp`, `QBS.Analysis.ExpLower`, `QBS.Analysis.NegExp`
The exponential on `[0,∞]` (groundwork for `e^{−x}` and exponential distributions).
- `⨆-*-lub`, `diag`, `tri`, `rect`, `cauchy` (`Cauchy`) — `module Cauchy A`: `(∑ᶠ a) * (∑ᶠ b) ≈ ∑ᶠ (n ↦ diag g n 0)`, the Cauchy product of two series, with `g i j = a i * b j`.
- `exp`, `ι`, `term`, `exp-mono`, `exp-cong`, `exp-zero`, `1+≤exp`, `exp-add` (`Exp`) — `module Exp A`, `module WithInv inv inv-ok`: `exp x = ∑ᶠ (xⁿ/n!)` given reciprocals `inv n * natE (n+1) ≈ 1`; `exp (x + y) ≈ exp x * exp y`; also `exp≤3 : x ≤ 1 → exp x ≤ 3`, `exp≤three^ : x ≤ natE N → exp x ≤ 3ᴺ` and `exp≤quad : x ≤ 1 → exp x ≤ 1 + (x + x·x)`.
- `expC`, `expC-zero`, `expC-mono`, `expC-cong`, `1+≤expC`, `expC≤quad`, `qC-mul`, `pow3`, `expC-bound` (`ExpLower`) — the instance at lower cuts, with `inv n = qC (1/(n+1))`; `expC-bound : x ⊑ natE N → ∀ s ∈ expC x, s < pow3 N` (`3ᴺ`).
- `negExp`, `negExp-anti`, `negExp-zero`, `negExp≤1`, `negExp-expC≤1`, `near-max`, `oneC≤negExp-expC`, `negExp-expC`, `negExp-expC-nat`, `Meas-expC`, `Meas-negExp` (`NegExp`, `module NegExp lem`) — `e^{−x}` as a cut: `q ∈ negExp x` iff `∃ p > q, ∀ s ∈ expC x, p·s < 1`; antitone, `negExp 0 ≋ 1`, `negExp x ⊗ expC x ⊑ 1`, and (`oneC≤negExp-expC`, `negExp-expC`) `negExp x ⊗ expC x ≋ 1` whenever `expC x` is bounded by a rational `B` (`∀ s ∈ expC x, s < B`), and unconditionally for `x ⊑ natE N` (`negExp-expC-nat`); measurable in `x`.

### `QBS.Analysis.Signed`, `QBS.Analysis.SignedOmega`, `QBS.Analysis.SignedIntegral`, `QBS.Analysis.Variance`
Signed values and signed integrals (plan 2, step 1). No cancellation or classical principle in `Signed`.
- `SubSemiring` (`Signed`) — a subsemiring of "finite" elements; `Signed` — `module Signed R F`: the ring of differences of finite elements (Grothendieck construction).
- `Sg` — a signed value `pos − neg` with finite parts; `_≃_` equality (`p + n′ + k ≈ p′ + n + k` for finite `k`); `_+S_`, `_*S_`, `-S_`, `0S`, `1S`, `ι` (embedding), `signedRing` (a `CommutativeRing`), `ι-+`, `ι-*`.
- `Bounded` (`SignedOmega`) — `module Bounded A`: finite = bounded by a natural number (`Bnd`, `nat`); re-exports `Signed` at that subsemiring.
- `Var pm f b`, `var-formula` (`Variance`) — variance `E[(f−E f)²]` of a bounded signed function, and `Var f ≃ E[f²] − (E f)²` (`_*SF_[_]` multiplies bounded signed functions).
- `SF m` (`SignedIntegral`) — a signed integrable function against `m`: measurable parts `f⁺`, `f⁻` with bounded integrals; `E` — its signed integral; `_≈SF_`, `E-cong`.
- `_+SF_`, `E-+` — sum and additivity; `-SF_`, `E-neg`; `scaleSF`, `E-scale` — scaling by a signed constant; `constSF`, `E-const`, `J-const` — constants against a probability measure.

### `QBS.Continuous.Monad`, `QBS.Continuous.ProbInstance`, `QBS.Continuous.SubProb`, `QBS.Continuous.RealModel`
- `Continuous` (`Monad`) — `module Continuous A lem K` (value semiring, `lem`, measure class such as `Integration.probClass`): the probability monad: `Pre`, `mk`, `∫`, `joinPre`, `∫-join`.
- `ContinuousProb` (`ProbInstance`) — it is a `ProbabilityInterface`; `continuousQBSLike`; `RealModel`: `realQBSLike`.
- `ContinuousSub` (`SubProb`) — the sub-probability monad; `scoreH` — soft conditioning by a measurable likelihood in `[0,1]`.
- `QBS.Analysis.Cone` (`Cone A`) — measures as a cone: `zeroM`, `scaleM c c≤1 μ`, `addM μ₁ μ₂ (mass μ₁ + mass μ₂ ≤ 1)`; `Mixture.mix` is `addM` of two `scaleM`s.

### `QBS.Continuous.SignedTests`, `QBS.Inference.ExpectationResp`, `QBS.Inference.Expectation`, `QBS.Inference.ExpectationProduct`, `QBS.Inference.ExpectationInt`, `QBS.Inference.ExpectationIntProduct`, `QBS.Inference.ExpectationExamples`, `QBS.Inference.BoolDist`, `QBS.BoolQ`, `QBS.Inference.CoinUniformPrior`, `QBS.Inference.CoinPosterior`
Expectations of bounded signed statistics under the monad's measures (module parameter `lem₁ : LEM₁`).
- `STest X` (`SignedTests`) — a bounded signed test `pos − neg` (parts `Hom X CutQ`, common natural bound); `pullback`; `E-at p g` (`ExpectationResp`) — its expectation under `p : Pre X`, a signed value; `E-resp` — `p ≈ᵖ q → E-at p g ≃ E-at q g`.
- `_+T_`, `-T_`, `scaleT c`, `constT c`, `precompT f`, `valT g x` (`Expectation`) — the algebra of signed tests.
- `E-at-η` — `E-at (ηPre x) g ≃ valT g x`; `E-at-map` — `E-at (mapPre f p) g ≃ E-at p (precompT f g)`.
- `E-at-+`, `E-at-neg`, `E-at-scale`, `E-at-const` — linearity in the test and constants.
- `expT g`, `E-at-expT`, `E-at-join` — `q ↦ E-at q g` as a signed test on `TQ X`, and the tower law `E-at (joinPre P) g ≃ E-at P (expT g)`.
- `f ⊗T g`, `valT-⊗T` (`ExpectationProduct`) — the product of signed tests on `X ×Q Y`, with `valT (f ⊗T g) (x , y) ≃ valT f x *S valT g y`; `E-at-cong` — pointwise `≃` tests have `≃` expectations.
- `E-at-σ` — `E-at (σPre (x , q)) (f ⊗T g) ≃ valT f x *S E-at q g`; `p ⊗ᵖ q` — the product measure `joinPre (mapPre (σq q) p)`; `E-at-⊗` — independence, `E-at (p ⊗ᵖ q) (f ⊗T g) ≃ E-at p f *S E-at q g`.
- `ITest p`, `E-atᴵ p g`, `fromSTest`, `E-atᴵ-fromSTest`, `truncHom`, `J-resp∞`, `transportI`, `E-respᴵ`, `_+I_`, `-I_`, `scaleI`, `E-atᴵ-+`, `E-atᴵ-neg`, `E-atᴵ-scale`, `valI`, `E-atᴵ-η`, `precompI`, `E-atᴵ-map` (`QBS.Inference.ExpectationInt`) — expectations of integrable (possibly unbounded) signed statistics: `J-resp∞ : p ≈ᵖ q → J (m p) (h ∘ α p) ≈ J (m q) (h ∘ α q)` (via the truncations `truncHom h k`), `E-respᴵ : p ≈ᵖ q → E-atᴵ p g ≃ E-atᴵ q (transportI e g)`.
- `Jp p h`, `J-join`, `J-σ`, `J-⊠`, `J-prodH`, `_⊗I_`, `E-atᴵ-⊗` (`ExpectationIntProduct`) — independence for integrable tests: `J-join` is the unbounded `∫-join` (Tonelli for `joinPre`), `J-⊠ : Jp (p ⊗ᵖ q) (mulX u v) ≈ Jp p u * Jp q v` for unbounded `u, v ≥ 0`, `f ⊗I g : ITest (p ⊗ᵖ q)` (parts `f⁺g⁺ + f⁻g⁻`, `f⁺g⁻ + f⁻g⁺`), and `E-atᴵ-⊗ : E-atᴵ (p ⊗ᵖ q) (f ⊗I g) ≃ E-atᴵ p f *S E-atᴵ q g`.
- `idT`, `sqT : STest AQ`, `E-at-uniform-id`, `E-at-uniform-sq`, `bern : Pre AQ`, `E-at-bern`, `E-at-bern²`, `E-at-bern²-quarter` (`QBS.Inference.ExpectationExamples`) — worked examples: `E-at uniform idT ≃ ι ½`, `E-at uniform sqT ≃ ι m` (`3m = 1`), Bernoulli(½) as the first bit of the fair coin with `E-at bern idT ≃ ι ½`, and independence `E-at (bern ⊗ᵖ bern) (idT ⊗T idT) ≃ ι ½ *S ι ½` (four copies add up to 1).
- `BoolQ`, `boolQ`, `trueQ`, `falseQ`, `constQ`, `ifQ a b` (`QBS.BoolQ`) — the Boolean QBS `1Q +Q 1Q` and case analysis `ifQ a b : Hom BoolQ Z` (the `Colimits.copair` of two constants); unlike `CodiscQ Bool` its indicators are measurable.
- `bitB`, `bernB : Pre BoolQ`, `ifT a b`, `indT : STest BoolQ`, `E-at-bernB-if`, `E-at-bernB-ind`, `toAQ`, `bernB≈bern`, `E-at-bernB-id`, `indB`, `bernθ θ`, `E-at-bernθ` (`QBS.Inference.BoolDist`) — Bernoulli(½) on `BoolQ` with `E-at bernB (ifT a b) ≃ ½a + ½b`, `E-at bernB indT ≃ ι ½`, `mapPre toAQ bernB ≈ᵖ bern`; the biased coin `bernθ θ` (the indicator `[expansion r ≤ θ]`, `indB` for any decidable Borel set) has `E-at (bernθ θ) indT ≃ ι θ` for `θ ≤ 1`.
- `Below2-borel`, `flipS`, `coinKernel : Model AQ BoolQ`, `marg`, `E-at-marg`, `coinKernel²`, `E-at-kernel²`, `marg²`, `E-at-marg²`, `HH-from-H` (`QBS.Inference.CoinUniformPrior`) — the coin with a uniform prior on its bias: `coinKernel θ = bernθ θ` is a morphism (measurability in θ from the variable-threshold Borel set `Below2-borel`), `E-at (joinPre (mapPre coinKernel uniform)) indT ≃ ι ½` (P(heads) = ∫ θ dθ), and for two conditionally independent flips `E-at marg² (indT ⊗T indT) ≃ ι m` with `3m = 1` (P(HH) = ∫ θ² dθ), multiplied out `3·P(HH) = 2·P(H)`.
- `posterior`, `massP-rw`, `bayes`, `post1`, `post1-mean`, `E-at-post1-id³`, `post2-seq`, `post2-batch`, `post2-agree` (`QBS.Inference.CoinPosterior`) — Bayes' rule by reweighting and normalising: `posterior ℓ p n h = normPre (reweightPre ℓ (ιPre p)) n h` with `∫ f d(posterior) ≈ n · ∫ ℓ·f dp`; after one head under the uniform prior, `post1` is Beta(2,1) with `3 · E[θ | H] ≈ 2`; updating on H then H (`post2-seq`) is `≈ᵖ` updating on HH at once (`post2-batch`).

### `QBS.Inference.Sampler`, `QBS.Inference.UniformSampler`
Statistical models as samplers (module parameter `lem₁ : LEM₁`; the `Sampler` module of `Inference/Sampler`).
- `Sampler Θ X` — a joint probe `Θ × ℛ ⇒ X`; `draw s θ`, `draw-random`, `single s g θ` — the bounded signed statistic of one draw on the fair coin; `obsAt`, `iidExperiment s g` — the iid experiment over the coordinates of the coin.
- `SingleMoments s g μ σ²` (`MeanAt`, `SecondAt`), `iid-moments`, `sampler-mean-unbiased` — the moments of one draw give those of the iid sample, so the unbiasedness theorems of `Inference/Frequentist` hold for every sampler.
- `Model Θ X`, `lawAt`, `samplerModel`, `E-at-sampler` — the kernel `Θ ⇒ TQ X` of a sampler; its `E-at` is the expectation of the single draw.
- `uniformSampler`, `idTest`, `singleMoments`, `moments`, `sample-mean-unbiased`, `sample-variance-unbiased` (`QBS.Inference.UniformSampler`) — validation: the uniform distribution as a sampler has the single-draw moments `E u`, `Var u`.

### `QBS.Inference.BernoulliCompare`
Bayesian vs frequentist on "is the coin fair or two-headed?" (module parameter `lem₁ : LEM₁`; `CoinBias` is used directly).
- `headSampler t : Sampler (1Q S) AQ` — the draw `[expansion r ≤ t]`; `twoHeadedS`, `fairS` (t = 1, ½), `sampler h`, `thr h`.
- `single-draw h`, `likelihood-is-expectation h` — `E-B fairCoin _ (single (sampler h) idTest _) ≃ ι (heads h)`: `E[head | two-headed] ≃ 1`, `E[head | fair] ≃ ½` (from `UniformCDF.cdf`).
- `evidence-is-prior-expectation` — `ι ½ *S E[head | two-headed] +S ι ½ *S E[head | fair] ≃ ι (½·1 + ½·½)`, the evidence of `CoinBias.evidence-heads` as the prior-weighted frequentist expectation.

### `QBS.Continuous.SFinite`, `QBS.Continuous.SFiniteStat`
- `∫ᴹ`, `_≈ᴹ_` (`SFinite`) — weighted integral and equality of s-finite measures.
- `continuousStat` (`SFiniteStat`, `module SFiniteStat lem`) — the complete `StatisticalInterface` for the continuous model.

### `QBS.Continuous.HalfLine`, `QBS.Continuous.HalfLineNegExp`, `QBS.Continuous.HalfLineUnit`, `QBS.Continuous.HalfLineNorm`, `QBS.Continuous.HalfLineMean`, `QBS.Continuous.HalfLineMoment2`, `QBS.Analysis.GeometricNorm`, `QBS.Analysis.GeometricMoment2`
Lebesgue measure on [0,∞) as an s-finite measure on the weight space `WQ = [0,∞]` (module parameter `lem₁ : LEM₁`).
- `shiftPt`, `shiftPre n`, `lebPre`, `Meas-shift` (`HalfLine`) — `shiftPre n` is the uniform on [0,1] shifted to `n` (probe `r ↦ (1, n + expansion r)` over the fair coin); `lebPre = sumPre shiftPre` is Lebesgue measure on [0,∞), `λ = ∑ₙ (n + U)`.
- `Meas-hshift`, `∫ᴹ-shift`, `∫ᴹ-leb` (`HalfLine`) — `∫ᴹ lebPre h ≈ ∑ᶠ n. J fairCoin (r ↦ h (n + expansion r))` for every morphism `h : WQ ⇒ WQ` (unbounded tests included).
- `negExpH`, `negExp-add`, `∫ᴹ-leb-negExp`, `∫ᴹ-leb-negExp-split`, `I-negExp` (`HalfLineNegExp`) — the test `x ↦ e^{−x}` as a morphism `WQ ⇒ WQ`; `e^{−(x+y)} ≈ e^{−x} e^{−y}` when `x`, `y`, `x+y` are bounded by naturals; `∫ e^{−x} dλ ≈ ∑ᶠ n. e^{−n} · I-negExp` with `I-negExp = ∫₀¹ e^{−u} du`.
- `G`, `F`, `q`, `F-split`, `xs-rec`, `I-negExp-unit` (`HalfLineUnit`) — `∫₀¹ e^{−u} du + e^{−1} ≈ 1` (no subtraction), by self-similarity: `F j = ∫₀¹ e^{−2⁻ʲ u} du` satisfies `F j ≈ ½ (e^{−2⁻⁽ʲ⁺¹⁾} F (j+1)) + ½ F (j+1)` (first-bit split and `negExp-add`).
- `negExp-pw`, `gb`, `norm-from`, `∫ᴹ-leb-negExp-norm` (`HalfLineNorm`) — `e^{−n} ≈ gⁿ`, and `∫ᴹ lebPre negExpH ≈ 1#`, i.e. `∫₀^∞ e^{−x} dx = 1`.
- `xNegExpH`, `KK`, `KK-split`, `ww-rec`, `K-pin`, `shift-split`, `∫ᴹ-leb-xNegExp` (`HalfLineMean`) — the mean `∫ᴹ lebPre xNegExpH ≈ 1#`, i.e. `∫₀^∞ x e^{−x} dx = 1`, for `xNegExpH : x ↦ x e^{−x}`.  With `K = ∫₀¹ u e^{−u} du`: `K-pin : K + (g + g) ≈ 1#` (self-similarity of `Kⱼ = ∫₀¹ u e^{−2⁻ʲu} du`, via `wⱼ = hⱼ²Kⱼ + hⱼqⱼ` and `GeometricNorm.norm-s`), `shift-split : ∫₀¹ (n+u) e^{−(n+u)} du ≈ e^{−n} (n I + K)`, then `GeometricNorm.GeoMean.total`.
- `x²NegExpH`, `LL`, `LL-split`, `x2-rec`, `L-pin`, `shift-split`, `∫ᴹ-leb-x²NegExp` (`HalfLineMoment2`) — the second moment `∫ᴹ lebPre x²NegExpH ≈ 1# + 1#`, i.e. `∫₀^∞ x² e^{−x} dx = 2`, for `x²NegExpH : x ↦ x² e^{−x}`.  With `L = ∫₀¹ u² e^{−u} du`: `L-pin : L + (((g + g) + (g + g)) + g) ≈ 1# + 1#` (self-similarity of `Lⱼ = ∫₀¹ u² e^{−2⁻ʲu} du`: the combination `x2 j = ½ hⱼ³ Lⱼ + (hⱼ + ½hⱼ²) qⱼ` has the recursion `x2-rec`, from the pins of `HalfLineUnit` and the mean at every scale, and `GeometricNorm.norm`), `shift-split : ∫₀¹ (n+u)² e^{−(n+u)} du ≈ e^{−n} (n² I + 2n K + L)`, then `GeoMom2.total`.
- `ss`, `two`, `U-inv`, `L-inv`, `base`, `norm`, `Geo.total` (`GeometricNorm`, `module GeometricNorm A R`, abstract in any `OmegaSemiring` with `Arch`) — `norm`: the recursion `x m ≈ (1 + q (m+1)) x (m+1)`, `q m ≈ q (m+1)²`, `q m ≤ 1`, the one-step facts (`base`) and `hp m q m ≤ x m ≤ hp m` give `x 0 + q 0 ≈ 1`, by error-doubling invariants and `arch`; `Geo.total`: `I + g ≈ 1` and `gᴺ (N+1) ≤ 1` give `(∑ᶠ gⁿ) I ≈ 1`.
- `Lsplit` (`abstract`, over `Lsplit′`, with the unfolding `Lsplit-def`), `x2-rec-alg`, `x2-lo`, `x2-hi`, `L5-alg`, `GeoMom2.total` (`GeometricMoment2`, `module GeometricMoment2 A R`, abstract) — the ring identities of the first-bit split of `L`, the recursion for `x2`, its bounds `h q ≤ x2 ≤ h`, and `GeoMom2.total`: `I + g ≈ 1`, `K + 2g ≈ 1`, `L + 5g ≈ 2` and `gᴺ (N+1) ≤ 1` give `∑ᶠ gⁿ (n² I + 2n K + L) ≈ 2` (the telescoping error `pol N gᴺ`, `pol N = N² + 2N + 2`, is small along `N = 4t`).

### `QBS.Continuous.SFiniteReweight`, `QBS.Continuous.ExpDist`, `QBS.Continuous.ExpDistSq`, `QBS.Inference.ExpDistMean`, `QBS.Inference.ExpDistVar`, `QBS.Inference.ExpDistPair`

- `mulH`, `reweightPre`, `∫ᴹ-reweight` (`SFiniteReweight`) — reweighting `p : Pre (XW X)` by a density `d : X ⇒ WQ`: `∫ᴹ (reweightPre d p) f ≈ ∫ᴹ p (mulH d f)` with `mulH d f` the pointwise product.
- `expW`, `∫ᴹ-expW`, `massP-expW`, `expDist`, `∫-expDist`, `∫-expDist-one`, `idW`, `∫-expDist-id` (`ExpDist`) — the exponential distribution: `expW = reweightPre negExpH lebPre`, `massP expW ≈ 1#`, `expDist = normPre expW 1# _ : C.Pre WQ`, `∫ᴹ (ιPre expDist) f ≈ ∫ᴹ lebPre (mulH negExpH f)`, total mass `1#` and mean `∫ᴹ (ιPre expDist) idW ≈ 1#`.
- `idC`, `zeroC`, `idI`, `E-atᴵ-idI` (`ExpDistMean`) — `idI : ITest expDist` (`pos = id`, `neg = 0`) and `E-atᴵ expDist idI ≃ 1`, so `E-atᴵ-map`, `E-respᴵ` and linearity of `ExpectationInt` apply to the exponential distribution.
- `sqW`, `∫-expDist-sq` (`ExpDistSq`) — the second moment `∫ᴹ (ιPre expDist) sqW ≈ 1# + 1#` for the squaring statistic `sqW : x ↦ x·x`.
- `sqC`, `sqI`, `E-atᴵ-sqI`, `var-expDist` (`ExpDistVar`) — `sqI : ITest expDist` (`pos = x²`, `neg = 0`), `E-atᴵ expDist sqI ≃ 2`, and the variance `E-atᴵ expDist sqI +S (-S (E-atᴵ expDist idI *S E-atᴵ expDist idI)) ≃ 1` (`E[X²] − (E X)² = 1`; `Analysis/Variance.Var` needs a bounded function, so the difference is stated directly as a signed value).
- `expPair`, `oneI`, `E-XY`, `E-X+Y`, `E-XYsq` (`ExpDistPair`) — two independent exponential draws `expPair = expDist ⊗ᵖ expDist`: `E[X·Y] ≃ 1`, `E[X + Y] ≃ 2`, and `E[X² + Y² + 2·X·Y] ≃ 6` (the second moment of `(X + Y)²`, written in its expanded form).

### Distributions: `QBS.Continuous.Uniform`, `QBS.Continuous.UniformMean`, `QBS.Continuous.UniformMoment`, `QBS.Continuous.UniformVariance`, `QBS.Continuous.UniformCDF`, `QBS.Continuous.SubModel`
